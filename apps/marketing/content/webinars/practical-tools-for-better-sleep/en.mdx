## What you will learn

Poor sleep and work stress feed each other. This recorded session gives you concrete tools to break the cycle -- techniques you can try tonight, not lifestyle overhauls that take months.

You will learn why stress disrupts sleep at a physical level, what actually helps (and what popular advice does not), how to build a wind-down routine that fits a busy schedule, and when it is worth speaking to a professional. The session is based on evidence-informed approaches used in clinical practice, explained in plain language.

## Who this is for

Anyone who sleeps badly -- whether it is trouble falling asleep, waking in the night, or waking up exhausted. You do not need a diagnosis or a history of sleep problems. If stress has been affecting your rest, this session is for you.

A 45-minute recorded session with a licensed clinical psychologist from the NestSelf network. Watch at your own pace. The recording is available in Serbian, English and Hungarian.
