## What you will learn

Loan offers are written to look attractive, not to be understood. This recorded session cuts through the jargon and shows you exactly what to look at when comparing offers.

You will learn the difference between nominal and effective interest rates and why it matters, how to read the total repayable amount, what fixed and variable rates mean for your monthly payment, how early repayment clauses work, and the red flags that should make you pause. The session uses real examples with numbers typical for Serbia and Hungary.

## Who this is for

Anyone considering a personal loan, a car loan, a home improvement loan or any form of credit. Also useful if you already have a loan and want to understand your terms better, or if you are thinking about refinancing. No financial background is needed.
