## What you will learn

Workplace stress is not always dramatic. It builds quietly – in the extra hour you stay, the sleep you skip, the irritability you brush off. This webinar helps you recognise those early signals and respond before they turn into burnout.

We will cover the difference between normal pressure and chronic stress, the physical and emotional signs most people miss, and simple, research-informed techniques you can start using the same week. You will also hear how to talk about stress at work without feeling exposed.

## Who this is for

This session is for anyone who works – whether you manage people or not. If you have been feeling drained, flat or short-tempered and want to understand why, this is a good place to start. Managers will find the section on spotting stress in their teams especially useful.

## Format

A 60-minute live session with a licensed clinical psychologist from the NestSelf network. The first 40 minutes are a guided presentation with real examples. The last 20 minutes are open for your questions – you can ask anonymously through the chat. The session is held in Serbian and English.
