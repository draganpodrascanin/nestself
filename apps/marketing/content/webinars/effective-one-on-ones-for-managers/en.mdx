## What you will learn

Most one-on-ones fail because they become status updates. This session shows you how to create a space where your direct reports actually tell you what is going on -- the workload problems, the frustrations, the early signs that someone is struggling.

We will cover how to structure the meeting so it belongs to the other person, the questions that open honest conversation, how to listen when someone shares something difficult, and what follow-through looks like in practice. You will also learn how to recognise when a team member needs professional support and how to point them toward it without overstepping.

## Who this is for

Managers, team leads and anyone responsible for other people at work. Whether you are new to management or have years of experience, this session focuses on practical technique, not theory.

## Format

A 60-minute live session with a clinical psychologist from the NestSelf network who specialises in workplace dynamics and leadership support. The first 40 minutes cover the method with worked examples, followed by 20 minutes of open questions. You can ask anonymously through the chat. The session is held in English and Serbian.
