## What you will learn

Employment contracts are full of terms that sound formal and feel hard to question. This recorded session walks through the sections that matter most – in plain language, without legal jargon.

You will learn what a probation clause actually means for you, how to read notice periods and termination conditions, what non-compete and confidentiality clauses can and cannot do, and when to ask for changes before signing. The session covers general principles relevant to both Serbia and Hungary, with notes on where the two countries differ.

## Who this is for

Anyone who is about to sign an employment contract, has recently started a new job, or wants to understand the contract they already signed. No legal background needed. The session is also useful for HR professionals who want to explain contracts more clearly to new hires.
