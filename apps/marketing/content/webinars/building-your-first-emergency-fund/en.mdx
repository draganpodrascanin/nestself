## What you will learn

An emergency fund sounds sensible in theory, but building one on a regular salary can feel impossible. This webinar breaks it down into realistic steps that work whether you earn a lot or a little.

You will learn how to calculate your target amount, find money in your current spending without a strict budget, automate your savings so you do not have to think about it, and protect the fund once it exists. We will also cover what to do if you have existing debt and how to balance saving with repayment.

## Who this is for

Anyone who does not yet have a financial safety net – or who started one and gave up. No financial background is required. The session uses plain language and real numbers from everyday life in Serbia and Hungary.

## Format

A 45-minute live session with a financial consultant from the NestSelf network. The presentation walks through a complete savings plan, followed by 15 minutes of audience questions. You can ask anonymously through the chat. The session is held in Serbian, Hungarian and English.
