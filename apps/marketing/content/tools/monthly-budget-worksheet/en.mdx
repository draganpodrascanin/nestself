# Monthly budget worksheet

Getting a clear picture of your money each month is the first step toward feeling more in control. Use this table to list what comes in, what goes out, and what is left.

## How to use this worksheet

1. Fill in your income for the month.
2. List your fixed expenses (rent, utilities, loan payments, insurance).
3. List your variable expenses (groceries, transport, personal spending).
4. Subtract your total expenses from your total income.

If the balance is negative, look for variable expenses you could reduce. If the balance is positive, consider setting aside a small amount as a buffer for unexpected costs.
