# Conversation planner

Talking to your manager about how you are feeling at work can feel daunting. This planner helps you organise your thoughts so the conversation is clear and constructive.

## Before you start

Choose a quiet moment when neither of you is rushed. This is a planning tool, not a script – adapt it to your situation and your relationship with your manager.

## Step 1: Name what you want to talk about

Write one or two sentences describing the topic. Be specific: "I have been finding it hard to manage my workload since the team changed" is clearer than "I am stressed."

**My topic:**

> _Write your topic here._

## Step 2: Describe what you have noticed

List concrete examples. Facts are easier to discuss than feelings alone.

- What has changed recently?
- How is it affecting your work?
- How is it affecting you outside work?

**My observations:**

> _Write your observations here._

## Step 3: Think about what would help

Before the conversation, think about what a good outcome looks like for you. It might be a change to your schedule, a clearer set of priorities or just knowing your manager is aware.

**What would help:**

> _Write what would help here._

## Step 4: Prepare your opening line

Having a first sentence ready makes it easier to begin. For example: "I would like to talk about how things have been going since the restructure. I have a few observations and a suggestion."

**My opening line:**

> _Write your opening line here._

## During the conversation

- Stick to your observations and your suggestion.
- Listen to your manager's perspective.
- If the conversation goes in an unexpected direction, it is fine to say "Let me think about that and come back to you."

## After the conversation

Note what was agreed and any follow-up steps. If you need more support, you can book a confidential session with a psychologist through NestSelf. Your employer never learns who used NestSelf or what was discussed.
