# Legal consultation checklist

A legal consultation works best when you come prepared. Use this checklist to gather what you need before your session.

## Before your consultation

- [ ] **Write down your main question.** What is the one thing you most need answered? Be as specific as you can: "Can my landlord increase the rent mid-lease?" is clearer than "I have a problem with my landlord."

- [ ] **Gather relevant documents.** Bring or have ready any contracts, letters, emails or court documents related to your situation. If you have a lease, employment contract or written agreement, have a copy to hand.

- [ ] **Note the key dates.** Write down when things happened – when you signed a contract, when you received a notice, when a deadline falls. Timelines matter in legal questions.

- [ ] **List the people involved.** Who are the other parties? Note their names, roles and how they are involved.

- [ ] **Think about what outcome you want.** Do you want to resolve the situation, understand your rights, or know what your options are? This helps the adviser focus the session.

## During your consultation

- [ ] **Take notes.** Write down the adviser's answers and any steps they suggest.

- [ ] **Ask about deadlines.** If there is a legal deadline for action, make sure you know when it is.

- [ ] **Ask about next steps.** What should you do after the session? Are there forms to file, letters to send or further documents to gather?

- [ ] **Ask about costs.** If further legal work is needed beyond what your programme covers, ask what it would involve and cost.

## After your consultation

- [ ] **Review your notes** while the conversation is fresh.

- [ ] **Complete any action items** the adviser recommended, especially anything with a deadline.

- [ ] **Book a follow-up** if the adviser suggested one or if new questions come up.
