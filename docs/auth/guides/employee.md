# Employee guide

Who this is for: employees whose organisation uses NestSelf. The pictures show fictional sample people.

---

## Sign in with a code

1. Open NestSelf, type your work email address and press **Continue**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-in.png" alt="Sign in" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

2. Type the code from the email NestSelf sent you and press **Verify**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-in-code.png" alt="Check your email" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

   **If it fails:** "The code you entered is not valid." Type the code from the newest email again, or press **Resend code**.

## Sign in through your organisation

1. Type your work email address on the same sign-in page and press **Continue**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-in-work-address.png" alt="Sign in with a work address" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

2. Sign in on your organisation's own sign-in page when NestSelf takes you there; you return to NestSelf signed in.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-in-sso-redirect.png" alt="Redirecting to your organisation's sign-in" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

   **If it fails:** "Your organisation has not enabled your account yet. Ask your HR administrator." Ask HR to invite you.

## Confirm your email when asked

1. When **Confirm your email address** appears, type the code NestSelf emailed you and press **Confirm**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-in-confirm-email.png" alt="Confirm your email address" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

   **If it fails:** "This confirmation link has expired. Sign in again." Press **Back to sign-in** and sign in again.

## Change your name and language

1. Open the menu with your name at the top right and choose **Settings**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-account-menu-settings.png" alt="Account menu, Settings" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

2. Type your name under **Display name** and press **Save name**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-settings.png" alt="Settings" width="100%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

3. Choose your language under **Language**.

## End sessions and sign out everywhere

1. In **Settings**, under **Signed-in sessions**, press **End session** next to a browser you do not recognise.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/workspace-settings-sessions.png" alt="Signed-in sessions" width="100%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

2. Press **End session** to confirm.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-end-session.png" alt="End this session?" width="65%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

3. To sign out on every device, press **Sign out everywhere**, then **Sign out everywhere** again.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-sign-out-everywhere.png" alt="Sign out everywhere?" width="65%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>

## Sign out

1. Open the menu with your name at the top right and choose **Sign out**.

   <p><img src="../screenshots/guide-workspace-account-menu-sign-out.png" alt="Account menu, Sign out" width="60%" style="break-before: avoid"></p>
