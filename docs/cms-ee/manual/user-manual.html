Writing and publishing articles

A tutorial for the content library: write an article, review it, publish it, and read it as an employee.

Milestone 1Edition of 30 October 2026
ReadersAuthors, reviewers, publishers, employees
ScreensFrom the automated test run, fictional organisations

An article passes through four people before an employee reads it. Each chapter of this tutorial follows one of them.

Author
Writes the article in the admin app in Serbian, Hungarian and English, then submits each language for review.
Reviewer
Approves each language, or returns it to the author with a comment.
Publisher
Publishes the approved article to its approved audience, in all four languages at once.
Employee
Reads it in the workspace app, in their own language, and saves it for later.

Who may do what

Your role comes from a grant (content_author, content_reviewer or content_publisher) for one or more categories. NestSelf staff assign grants in the admin app under Access.

Buttons you may not use are hidden, and the server checks every action again on its own.

About the screenshots

Every screen comes from the automated journey that tests these features (e2e/content/), run against a local API with fictional sample organisations and people. The admin app is shown in English; every screen works the same in Serbian and Hungarian.

Buttons and fields are written in bold green, for example Submit for review.

Create the article

Open Content and select Create resource. Choose the Primary category, the Review discipline and the Initial language you start in, type the Article title and select Create article. The editor opens on that language.

Start writing

The title sits at the top and the body is one writing area, with no toolbar. Properties holds the article's details; leave them for later.

Add blocks with “/”

Type / to open the block menu, keep typing to filter it (/num finds Numbered list), and press Enter. Esc closes it and keeps what you typed.

  • Professional note: a callout; pick Tip, Warning or Disclaimer.
  • Practical step: a numbered card with a Step title.
  • Checklist: Enter adds an item, Enter on an empty item ends the list.
Faster: type the shortcut

At the start of a line, ## and a space make a heading, ### a subheading, - a bulleted list, 1. a numbered list, > a quote and [] a checklist.

Format a selection

Select text to show the formatting bar: Bold, Link and Turn into, which changes the whole block (Ctrl+B and Ctrl+K work too; Cmd on a Mac). A link must be an https:// address on the allowed list or another NestSelf article; anything else is refused.

Move and change blocks

Hover a block: Add block (+) and a grip appear on its left. The + offers the same block types as /: pick one to turn that line into a heading, list or quote; an image, note or call to action replaces an empty line or goes below a filled one. Drag the grip to move the block up or down; a line shows where it lands. Click the grip for Turn into, Duplicate, Delete, Move up and Move down. From the keyboard: Ctrl+/ (Cmd+/ on a Mac) opens that menu, Alt+Shift+↑ and Alt+Shift+↓ move the block.

Add an image

Choose Image in the block menu and pick a file, or drop or paste one. The block shows “Uploading...” and “Processing...” until the image is ready. Alt text is required: a short description for people who cannot see the image.

End with a call to action

Call to action is the one button at the end of an article. Choose what it does (Suggest topic, Open content, Open collection or Open service), search for its target by title, optionally write a button label, and select Done. The block is added only when its target is set. Related resource adds a card for another article in the same way.

Fill in the properties

Open Properties: the Summary shown on cards, the Expected outcome, the reading time in minutes, the Proposed audience and the Sources. The audience is required before you submit; in this milestone it is All eligible.

Let it save

The editor saves a moment after you stop typing, and before you leave the page; the status reads “Saved”. If someone else saved the same draft meanwhile, saving stops and the editor says so: Reload server version continues from their copy.

Preview it

Preview shows the article as employees will see it, on a phone (390 px), a desktop (1440 px) or a small phone (320 px).

Write the other languages

An article is published in Serbian (Latin), Hungarian and English together. Open each language tab and create its variant. Serbian (Cyrillic) is produced from the Latin text and is read-only.

Submit for review

Select Submit for review on each of the three languages. A submitted revision is locked until a reviewer approves or returns it.

Return it with a comment

Open the article in Content and switch to the language you review. Return for changes sends it back to the author and needs a comment. You cannot review a revision you wrote or last edited, or an article outside your review discipline.

Tell the author directly for now

In this milestone the comment is recorded with the review but not yet shown to the author. It appears on the article in milestone 2.

Approve it

When the author has resubmitted, Approve accepts exactly that revision. Approve each of the three languages.

Publish now

When all three languages are approved, Publish now opens a confirmation with the audience the reviewers approved. Confirm it to publish.

Check that it is live

The article is live in all four languages at once. A later approved revision replaces it when you publish again.

Find it on Home

Home lists the articles available to your organisation, on a computer or a phone.

Read it

The article shows its summary, what you gain, the reading time, who reviewed it and when, and then the body with its notes, steps, checklists, images, related articles and the call to action at the end.

Read it in your language

Choose your Language under Settings. Serbian comes in Latin and in Cyrillic script.

Save it for later

Save this resource keeps the article under Saved.

Work for two organisations

If you work for two client organisations, switch between them at the top of the page. Each one shows only its own articles and saved list; an article of the other shows “This content is not available”

Revision states

Draft
The author is writing. Saved automatically.
In review
Submitted and locked until a reviewer decides.
Changes requested
Returned to the author, who edits and submits again.
Approved
Ready to publish once all three languages are approved.
