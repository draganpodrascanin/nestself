## Shared design decisions for this round

- **Rhythm.** Every marketing section uses the shared spacing constant `SECTION_PADDING = 'py-16 @max-[760px]:py-10'` exported from `apps/marketing/src/components/sections/section.ts` (new file, stream S). Heroes use `py-16 @max-[760px]:py-8`. Sections are left-aligned; no stray centred headings in an otherwise left-aligned page.
- **Hierarchy.** Section title (`Heading` level 2, default `sectionTitle`, or `editorial` for the home testimonials) > item titles (`sectionTitle` at level 3 only where the item is a real sub-section, otherwise `type-label`) > body (`tone="secondary"`). Metadata (`type-metadata`) is for dates, roles and counts only.
- **Containment.** Lists of peer items get cards (`Card` from `@nestself/ui`) or a timeline, never bare text blocks floating in whitespace.
- **Phones first.** Every change is looked at 390 px wide before 1440 px.
