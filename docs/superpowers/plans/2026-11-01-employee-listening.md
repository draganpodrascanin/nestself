# Employee Listening and Workplace Reviews Implementation Plan

> **For agentic workers:** REQUIRED SUB-SKILL: Use superpowers:subagent-driven-development (recommended) or superpowers:executing-plans to implement this plan task-by-task. Steps use checkbox (`- [ ]`) syntax for tracking.

**Goal:** Build the employee listening module of `docs/tasks/employee-listening/spec.md`: recurring five-question surveys with protected company and department reporting, an optional human workplace review that an employer explicitly requests, scopes, books and receives a human-written report for, and an employer-anonymous feedback channel with case handling; slices 1 to 7 of the spec's section 13 (P0 and the P0-IF gate), not the P1 extensions of slice 8.

**Architecture:** Three NestJS modules in `apps/api` with their own Drizzle tables under row-level security: `listening` (configuration, questionnaire versions, cycles, participation, finalization, aggregates, disclosure, reports, exports, the review offer and the BullMQ jobs), `feedback-cases` (the restricted organizational feedback domain, modelled on `professional`: confidential resources, CASE assignments, 404 on denial, reporter identity in a separate table) and `workplace-reviews` (requests, versioned scopes, engagements, sharing grants, human report versions, actions and follow-up, reusing `appointments` for the booked meeting and `@nestself/content` for the report document). Scoring, classification, freshness, trend and the disjoint-cohort suppression plan are one pure library in `@nestself/contracts`, consumed by the API (cards, heatmap, export, pack, offer) and shared with the web apps for formatting only; the clients never classify. Nineteen new actions and seven new permission templates extend the grant engine without changing it; the five `feedback_case.*` actions join `CONFIDENTIAL_ACTIONS`. The workspace app gains the employee survey, the feedback channel, the handler inbox and the employer listening area; the admin app gains questionnaire authoring, per-client configuration, the coordinator queue and the reviewer's engagement workspace. A new `listening` i18n namespace carries all copy in four locales.

**Tech Stack:** NestJS 12, Drizzle 0.45 on PostgreSQL 17 with RLS, BullMQ 6 (worker), zod 4 contracts, React 19 + TanStack Router and Query (workspace, admin), `@base-ui/react` through `@nestself/ui`, `@nestself/content` (Tiptap editor and renderer), Vitest, supertest, fast-check, Playwright. No new dependencies.

**Spec:** `docs/tasks/employee-listening/spec.md` (sections 4 and 5 hold the product rules and the privacy contract, 7 the stories, 8 and 9 the states and the domain model, 13 the slice order, 14 the golden fixtures). Decisions that depart from or settle the spec are recorded in `docs/adr/0002-employee-listening-module.md` (Task 1).

## Global Constraints

- No `pnpm install`, `pnpm add`, `npx`, `pnpm dlx` or anything that touches `pnpm-lock.yaml` or `node_modules`; no new dependencies, including workspace ones (the API does not depend on `@nestself/i18n`).
- Every route carries `@Public()`, `@Authenticated()` or `@Authorize()`; `default-deny.test.ts` must pass. Every new tenant table has RLS with `FORCE` (hand-added to the generated migration) and is listed in `test/db/schema.spec.ts`.
- Audit details are sanitised (`FORBIDDEN_KEYS`, `PINO_REDACT_CONFIG`): never a survey answer, a feedback body, a report section, a reporter identity or a participation link. No audit row names the reporter of a feedback case or the author of a survey response (`actorUserId: null`, `actorKind: 'SYSTEM'` for those events).
- No endpoint, list, export, pack, log or audit row lets an employer, reviewer, support or staff user resolve a survey response or a feedback case to a person; participation is never enumerated (spec 5.1, 5.3).
- Scores are integers in tenths of a percent (`scoreTenths`); the official score has one decimal, round half up (`59.95 -> 60.0`, `74.95 -> 75.0`); bands are `>= 750` good, `600..749` monitor, `< 600` danger; the minimum cohort is 10 distinct valid respondents per dimension per cohort and cannot be configured below 10; freshness is 45 days from `closesAt`; cadence 14 days, window 7 days, at most 2 reminders (spec 4.2, 4.3, 5.2).
- One suppression plan per snapshot, persisted with it and reused by cards, heatmap, detail, trend, export and pack; a suppressed value is `null`, never 0 (spec 5.2 step 5).
- Submission is accepted only while `opensAt <= server_accepted_at < closesAt` under the cycle's `FOR SHARE` lock; finalization takes `FOR UPDATE` and is retry-safe (spec 4.2).
- Every user-caused failure renders translated copy in `en`, `sr-Latn`, `hu` under the `listening` namespace; `sr-Cyrl` is generated (`pnpm --filter @nestself/i18n build:cyrillic`), never edited by hand; unknown codes fall back to `errors.unknown`; `pnpm check` must pass.
- Colour and motion rules: semantic tokens only (`success`, `warning`, `danger`, `neutral` tones), text labels beside every colour, any new popout animated per the Motion rule; no hex, no literal durations.
- Emails are sent after the transaction that justifies them has committed, never inside it, through `Mailer`; bodies are neutral (no score, answer, case text or report content); logs carry the template, the locale and the recipient domain only.
- Commits end with `Co-Authored-By: Claude Fable 5.1 <noreply@anthropic.com>`. Integration suites run with `TEST_DATABASE_URL=postgresql://nestself:nestself_dev@localhost:54329/nestself_test` (Postgres 17 on 54329, Redis on 6379). Before each commit: `pnpm --filter <package> lint && pnpm --filter <package> typecheck && pnpm --filter <package> test` for every package touched; `pnpm check` after any i18n, token or motion change.

## Review Focus

1. **A submission whose transaction is still committing when the finalizer starts.** Expected: the finalizer waits on the cycle lock, the answer is counted, a submission that reaches the lock at or after `closesAt` is refused with `cycle_closed`, and running the finalizer twice produces one snapshot. Test in Task 6 (`listening-finalize.e2e.spec.ts`).
2. **A department whose headcount is 15 but only 9 answered one dimension, next to a company total of 25 and a sibling of 16.** Expected: that cell is hidden, the sibling is hidden too (secondary suppression), the company shows, the export and the heatmap agree and the detail endpoint for the hidden cell returns the same neutral placeholder with no count. Test in Task 7 (`disclosure.property.test.ts`, `listening-reports.e2e.spec.ts`).
3. **An employee who ends their membership between invitation and reminder, or transfers departments mid-cycle.** Expected: no reminder is sent, no submission is accepted after the end, and an answer submitted before the end stays attributed to the launch-snapshot department. Test in Task 5.
4. **A feedback case routed to the alternate because the problem concerns the primary handler.** Expected: the primary receives no assignment, no mail and cannot open the case (404), the handler view carries a pseudonym and no reporter field at any depth, and a reporter reply on a resolved case reopens it with an owner. Test in Task 11.
5. **Two employer users confirming the same proposed window, or a reviewer whose sharing grant was revoked after assignment.** Expected: exactly one appointment is created, the second caller gets `slot_unavailable`; the pack returns 404 `sharing_revoked` semantics for the reviewer while the engagement stays readable, and no score changes through any review mutation. Test in Task 13 and Task 14.

- The suppression algorithm (spec 5.2): mark every child with `validCount < minimum` as `primary`; if the parent has `validCount < minimum` it is `hidden` and every child is at least `primary`; otherwise sum the hidden children's counts (the residual `unassigned` cohort is a child like any other) and while `0 < hiddenSum < minimum` hide the smallest `shown` child (ties by `cohortKey` ascending) as `secondary`; if no `shown` child remains and `0 < hiddenSum < minimum`, the parent is `hidden`.
