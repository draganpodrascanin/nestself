Overview

What this strategy decides

Fourteen of the thirty-two decisions carry the design. Each has a numbered entry, with conditions and alternatives, in the engineering reference.

A small content system inside the platform we already run. Every resource in all four languages. One availability rule decides what every employee sees, everywhere. Original employee text never enters the CMS.

C-02
A thin CMS inside the NestSelf API and admin, no headless CMS
One sign-in, one grant model, one audit log. A second system would need its own access rules.
C-03
Tiptap in the admin; content stored as validated structured blocks
Editors cannot insert HTML or scripts. Employees' screens render the blocks with NestSelf components, never raw HTML.
C-06
Approval binds one exact revision
A fingerprint covers text, media, links, call to action and audience. Any change after approval needs review again.
C-07
One shared catalogue; clients see it through audiences
No copies per client. Personal state (saved items, votes, requests) is per employer and isolated by row-level security.
C-08
Author, reviewer and publisher are separate grants
The existing content actions are reused; review moves out of the publisher template. Nobody approves their own work.
C-10
One availability decision for every read path
Home, lists, search counts, detail, saved items and media all ask the same question, so they cannot disagree or leak.
C-11
Client capabilities come from configuration, never from copy founder: per client
Library, general questions and event interest are switched on per client. Editors cannot promise a service.
C-14
Private media, scanned, re-checked on every request
No public file links. Revoking access works for the next byte range, not the next day.
C-15
Search in PostgreSQL, Serbian and Hungarian aware
Both stemmers ship with PostgreSQL. Cyrillic and Latin fold to one form. Counts and snippets only cover what the reader may see.
C-17
General questions live in the confidential domain
The confidential domain's own access paths: the asker, the professional who claimed it, or approved emergency access. Nobody else, whatever their role.
C-19
Poll votes are pseudonymous and never shown in small groups
One current answer per person per poll; ten distinct participants before any distribution appears.
C-20
Requests are tracked to an outcome; editors see a sanitized brief
Owners, due dates on a business calendar, and no raw employee text or identity in the editorial queue.
C-22
The employee context names its employer
A person with two employers has two separate sets of saved items, votes and requests. Switching clears every cached screen.
C-27
Employees and professionals use the workspace app; staff use the admin
Professionals are not staff. Their question queue sits beside the employee workspace, as the auth strategy places it.

Content

From draft to publication

One resource, one variant per language, immutable revisions, and publications that point at one approved revision.

Four records

Resource: the language-independent item (article, video, audio, practical resource, Q&A or collection) with its primary category and owner.

Variant: one language version with its own review.

Revision: a frozen snapshot once submitted; the one working draft autosaves and refuses to overwrite a newer change.

Publication: the approved revision, its audience and its time window.

  1. Author drafts

    Type, language, category, the promise to the reader ("what you get"), duration, sources, media and a proposed audience. Validation lists what is missing.

  2. Preview in the real layout

    The employee renderer at phone and desktop widths, for a simulated client and team; no real employee's state is loaded.

  3. Submit one revision for review

    A reviewer qualified for the discipline approves or returns it with comments. Authors and last editors cannot review their own revision.

  4. Publish now or schedule

    The publisher confirms the audience, which may narrow the approved one but never widen it. Only the approved fingerprint can go live.

  5. Maintain

    Review dates, rights expiry and collection minimums create tasks for an owner and a backup. Urgent withdrawal removes the item everywhere at once.

States
Revision
draft→in_review→changes_requested or approved→superseded
Publication
scheduled→live→expired, or withdrawn at any time
Media asset

Editing live content never shows half-reviewed textEmployees keep seeing the previous approved revision until the new one is approved and its publication starts. Starting a publication never waits for a background job: availability is computed from the stored times on every read.

Editorial scope is the primary category

The auth strategy's "assigned collections" scope is mapped to categories: an author assigned to Sleep and rest edits resources whose primary category is Sleep and rest. Lists are filtered to the categories a person holds the action for.

No administrator key to private questions

Platform, organization and category grants are refused by the decision engine for confidential records. A known record id gives an admin, HR or CMS account nothing.

Unavailable means not found

An employee who may not see an item gets a 404, not a refusal that confirms it exists. A saved item that was withdrawn shows a neutral "no longer available".

Two employers, two worlds

Saved items, history, votes and requests belong to one employer context. Switching employer clears every cached screen before the new one loads.

Employees

Home, Explore, Saved, My requests

Built mobile first in the workspace app. No mood questions, no streaks, no view counts.
Home, in this order, empty modules omitted
  1. Greeting and search, with the current employer visible
  2. One featured item with its outcome and duration
  3. Quick needs: "A calmer end to the day", "Better sleep"
  4. Under five minutes: three or four formats
  5. Saved and continue, when private state exists
  6. Explore topics: the six starter categories
  7. Collection of the month, curated
  8. Ask a professional: one approved answer
  9. Shape future topics: at most one poll
  10. Additional support: only what really works

Search in everyday language

Serbian Latin or Cyrillic, with or without diacritics, Hungarian, English; results always in the reader's language. Synonyms such as "I cannot switch off". Typing errors fall back to similarity matching. Raw search text is never stored.

Detail that works on a phone

Outcome, duration, author and reviewer with the review date, practical steps, transcripts and captions. Video never starts with sound. "Was this useful?" and one allowed next step.

Private by default

Up to three interests, optional and editable. Continuation history can be switched off, which deletes it. Nothing here infers a health condition.

Media

Files that only reach their audience

Local storage in development, a private S3 bucket in staging and production, both behind the same interface.
Upload
Staff stream a file through the API into a private quarantine area. Size is capped per kind before anything is read.
Check
The real file type is read from its bytes; the malware verdict comes from GuardDuty in AWS. SVG, HTML and executables are refused.
Process
Metadata such as GPS is stripped; images get phone and desktop renditions; audio and video are probed for length and rewritten for streaming.
Approve
Rights, licence, consent and alternative text sit on the asset. A revision with an unready or expired file cannot be submitted or published.
Deliver
Every request, including every byte range of a video, re-checks the reader's access. No public links, no shared caches.

Engagement

Polls, feedback and requests

Each signal has an owner and an outcome the employee can see.

Interest polls

  • One question, two to five options and "none of these"; open seven to fourteen days
  • Topic interest or format preference only; never a health question
  • One current answer per person, changeable until close; no history kept
  • Eligibility fixed at opening; new members join the next poll
  • At most one poll per person per week; a skipped poll stays away
  • Closing records a decision and, where possible, links what was published

What the employee is told

"Participation is optional. Your employer cannot see your individual response. NestSelf uses responses for aggregate content planning." The word anonymous is never used: votes are pseudonymous, keyed per poll with a secret the editors do not hold.

Editorial requests

Suggest a topic, another format or a translation, register interest in an event topic, or report a problem with a resource. Structured choices are enough to submit.

Questions

A general question reaches one professional

Professional answers to general educational questions. Not therapy, not a chat, not an emergency channel.
Who can open the original question
The employee who askedTheir question, clarifications and the approved reply
The professional who claimed itThrough the case assignment the claim creates; gone when reassigned
Time-limited emergency accessSeparately approved and logged, from the auth strategy
Everyone elsePlatform owner, CMS staff, client success, HR, managers: refused in code, even with the id

  1. Ask

    Subject, language and up to 1,500 characters. No attachments. The form says who reads it, that the employer does not, and where urgent help is.

  2. Claim

    Queues per discipline show only safe details. Claiming is atomic and creates the read path; the first read needs a fresh sign-in.

  3. Clarify, at most twice

    One clear question per round, so the flow never becomes an unpromised chat.

  4. Answer

    Private, attributed and dated, reviewed by a second professional where the subject requires it.

  5. Maybe publish a generalized version

    Only with the employee's yes to the exact text. Declining changes nothing about their answer.

Before claiming

The queue shows the discipline, language, waiting time in business hours, due date and clarification round. Never the question, never the asker's name, never the employer's name.

After claiming

The professional reads the question and replies privately. Every read is audited without copying the text. If the claim sits idle for two business days, it returns to the queue and the former reader loses access.

Decisions taken, and what remains

The founder answered the open product questions on 24 October 2026. Legal review remains before real employees use the library.

Every resource in all four languages decided

Readers always see their chosen language. Serbian Latin, Hungarian and English are written and reviewed; Cyrillic is generated from the approved Latin text, as the interface already is.

Effect: a resource publishes only when all three are approved; writing and review effort roughly triples.

Questions open only when staffed accepted

Each discipline needs a named owner and backup with weekly capacity.

Effect: the question form explains what is available until then.

Request metadata kept 12 months decided

The value is one setting in the retention table.

Effect: a command changes it without a deployment, and the change is audited.

Library on for every client accepted

General questions and event interest stay off per client until they are staffed.

Effect: client success switches them on later.

Schedule assumed

About ten calendar weeks to a pilot-ready V1, one more than the epic's estimate.

Default: keep V1 scope; accept the extra week.

Content capacity needed

Serbian, Hungarian and English writers and professional reviewers for about forty starter resources.

Needed by: M2, when the library opens to the first synthetic pilot.
