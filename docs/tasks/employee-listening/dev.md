## 1. The system in one picture

Three API modules behind the existing authorization pipeline, one shared scoring library, two web apps and four locales.

```
employee (personal context)        employer (organization context)         NestSelf staff / expert
  /survey                            /listening, /listening/heatmap,         admin /listening/questionnaires
  /feedback, /feedback/new           /listening/dimensions/:key,             admin /organizations/:id/listening
  /feedback/:caseId                  /listening/surveys, /listening/settings  admin /workplace-reviews[/:id]
                                     /listening/reviews[/new|/:id|/engagements/:id]
                                     /feedback/inbox[/:caseId], settings     admin /reviews/engagements[/:id]
        |                                   |                                        |
        v                                   v                                        v
  apps/api/src/listening  <----->  apps/api/src/feedback-cases  <----->  apps/api/src/workplace-reviews
  configuration, questionnaire     routes, cases, reporter link,        catalogue, requests, scopes,
  versions, cycles, participation, messages, escalation               engagements, sharing grants,
  finalization, aggregates,                                            report versions, actions,
  disclosure, reports, export,                                         notes, follow-up
  review offer, listening jobs
        \__________________________ @nestself/contracts/listening-scoring.ts __________________________/
                                  (scoreTenths, classifyScore, trendTenths, isStale, planSuppression)
```

A survey answer is written once, aggregated once at the close of its cycle into immutable per-cell rows, and read only through those rows. A feedback case lives in a confidential domain where the reporter's account is a separate table that no handler path joins. A workplace review is a commercial conversation (request, scope, acceptance) that never changes a score and never moves money inside the platform.

## 4. Actions, templates and step-up

Actions (`packages/contracts/src/actions.ts`): `listening.configure`, `listening.templates.write`, `listening.templates.publish`, `listening.cycles.manage`, `listening.respond.self`, `listening.reports.read`, `listening.reports.export`; `feedback_case.create.self`, `feedback_case.read.self`, `feedback_case.reply.self`, `feedback_case.handle`, `feedback_case.assign` (all five in `CONFIDENTIAL_ACTIONS`, D-EL-06); `workplace_reviews.request`, `workplace_reviews.coordinate`, `workplace_reviews.scope.accept`, `workplace_reviews.engagement.read`, `workplace_reviews.report.write`, `workplace_reviews.report.publish`, `workplace_reviews.actions.manage`.

Templates (`templates.ts`): `survey_administrator` (cycles and reports), `feedback_handler` (handles assigned cases; an `assigned_cases` scope through a CASE assignment on the case), `workplace_review_requester` (requests, follows, agrees actions), `workplace_review_approver` (accepts a scope; requires the `spending_ceiling` condition), `listening_administrator` (staff: configuration and questionnaires), `review_coordinator` (staff: the queue), `workplace_reviewer` (expert: report write and publish, `consultation.join` and `delivery.record` restricted by the `categories` condition to `workplace_review`, so a reviewer cannot join clinical consultations). The existing `employee` template gained `listening.respond.self`, `feedback_case.create.self`, `feedback_case.read.self` and `feedback_case.reply.self`; `hr_admin` and `reporting_user` gained `listening.reports.read`.

Step-up classes (`authorization.ts`): `listening.configure` and `listening.templates.publish` are `administration`, `listening.reports.export` is `export`, `feedback_case.handle` and `feedback_case.assign` are `restricted`, `workplace_reviews.scope.accept` is `financial`. The web apps wrap those calls in `withStepUp`.

Accepting a scope evaluates the approver's grant against a resource that carries `amountMinor` and `currency` from the scope version, so the `spending_ceiling` condition decides (D-EL-04). Assigning a reviewer runs the planned CASE assignment through the engine (`workplace_reviews.report.write` on the engagement with the `workplace_review` category) and refuses with `reviewer_not_qualified` when the candidate lacks the approved credential or the template (D-EL-11).

## 7. Scoring and disclosure contract

`packages/contracts/src/listening-scoring.ts` is the only implementation (D-EL-02); the API persists what it computes and the web apps format it.

- `scoreTenths(favorable, valid)`: `round_half_up(1000 × favorable / valid)` in integers, `null` for zero valid answers. `59.95 → 60.0`, `74.95 → 75.0` with no floating point.
- `classifyScore(tenths, { goodTenths, monitorTenths })`: `good` at or above 750, `monitor` at or above 600, `danger` below; the thresholds come from the cycle's policy version.
- `trendTenths(current, previous)`: the difference in tenths, only between cycles with compatible questionnaire versions (`comparableWithVersionId`); otherwise the trend says why it is unavailable (`no_previous`, `incompatible_version`, `suppressed`).
- `isStale(closesAt, freshnessDays, now)`: 45 days by default; a stale latest result shows gray `stale` on the overview, keeps its colour in history and never triggers the review offer.
- `planSuppression({ parentValid, children })`: a cell needs at least `minimumCohort` (platform floor 10, a client may raise it) valid answers for that dimension; when the hidden children of a parent total fewer than the minimum, the smallest visible siblings are hidden too so the hidden ones cannot be reconstructed (PR-01..PR-08); the `unassigned` residual takes part in the union. `disclosure.property.test.ts` checks the property on random trees.
- Gray reasons: `collecting`, `not_measured`, `insufficient_responses`, `privacy_suppressed`, and `stale` for the current overview.

Defaults (`DEFAULT_LISTENING_POLICY`): good 75.0, monitor 60.0, minimum cohort 10, freshness 45 days, cadence 14 days, response window 7 days, two reminders, algorithm version 1. A change writes a new `listening_policy_versions` row; the next cycle copies it.

The review offer (`review-offer.service.ts`, REV-01) is shown when the organization has workplace reviews enabled, the latest result is current, at least one company dimension is `danger`, no request or engagement for the scope is active, and the offer was not dismissed for this snapshot. It never books, charges or writes to the client.

## 8. Privacy mechanics

- **Responses carry no identity.** `survey_responses` has the cohort unit and the answers; `survey_participations` has the user and the state. Finalization reads responses only; the purge job deletes private drafts of closed cycles and old participation links, never responses or snapshots.
- **Aggregates leave through an allowlist.** `reports.service.ts` and `review-sharing.service.ts` map cells through one function that emits `score`, `status`, `grayReason`, `respondentCount` (only for a shown cell) and whole-percent distribution shares; hidden cells carry nulls. The CSV is built from the same cells (REP-04). `listening-dto-leak.e2e.spec.ts` walks every response of the three modules for `userId`, `membershipId`, `responseId`, `answers`, `validCount`, `favorableCount`, `reporterUserId`, `email` and the other keys of its lists; `listening-reports.e2e.spec.ts` checks PR-01 on a 25/20/5 tree and that the pack equals the heatmap rows.
- **The reporter is a separate table.** `feedback_case_reporters` is joined on the reporter's own routes only. Handler and employer views show the pseudonym (`Reporter-XXXX`, a Crockford base32 code with no relation to the account), the route function and the thread. Audit rows of reporter-driven events (`feedback_case.created`, `feedback_case.reporter_replied`) have `actorUserId: null` and `actorKind: 'SYSTEM'`. Notification mails name no reporter and carry no content.
- **The alternate route hides the case from the primary.** A case routed to the alternate is assigned to the alternate only; the primary gets no mail, no inbox row and a 404 on the case. The hourly escalation moves a case from the primary to the alternate when the response target passed, and never from the alternate back to the primary (D-EL-14).
- **Logs and audit.** `pino-redaction.ts` redacts `answers`, `body`, `sections` and `reporterExplanation` in request bodies and nested objects; `audit-writer.ts` strips `answers`, `reporterUserId`, `sections`, `internalNote`, `caseBody` and `reporterExplanation` from audit details. The access log already redacts `Location` and `Referer`.
- **Reviewer access is granted and revocable.** The pack route needs an active sharing grant; a revoked grant answers 409 `sharing_revoked` at once. Internal notes are readable on the expert routes only. The employer reads a report version only after the reviewer shared it.
- **Row-level security** on every tenant table as defense in depth (`db/listening-schema.spec.ts`, `db/feedback-schema.spec.ts`).

## 9. Jobs and notifications

| Job                         | Queue and schedule                                 | What it does                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                       |
| --------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| `listening.tick`            | `nestself-listening`, every 5 minutes              | Creates the next recurring cycle of every client whose launch is due, launches scheduled cycles whose start passed (audience snapshot, invitations), closes and finalizes cycles whose close passed: aggregates, applies suppression, writes one snapshot per cycle (idempotent: a second run finds the snapshot and does nothing), audits `listening.cycle.finalized` or `listening.cycle.failed` |
| `listening.remind`          | hourly                                             | At most `reminderLimit` neutral reminders per participation to members who have not submitted, never after submission or membership end                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                            |
| `listening.purge`           | 03:20                                              | Deletes drafts of closed cycles and participation links older than the retention window; never responses or snapshots                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                                              |
| `feedback.escalate-overdue` | `nestself-maintenance`, 30 minutes past every hour | Moves a case the primary let pass its response target to the alternate (when eligible) and tells them; a case on the alternate route is marked escalated and its handler reminded; audits `feedback_case.escalated`                                                                                                                                                                                |

`ListeningJobSchedulerService` registers the listening jobs on their own BullMQ queue with the same worker process as the maintenance jobs (`pnpm --filter @nestself/api worker:dev`); `MaintenanceJobsService` carries the escalation. `apps/api/src/worker/run-job.ts` runs the maintenance jobs by name; the listening jobs run through `ListeningJobsService.run(name)` in tests and are not yet wired into `run-job.ts` (section 13).

Notifications are emails (D-EL-05) sent after commit through the `Mailer` port: `survey_invitation`, `survey_reminder`, `feedback_case_assigned`, `feedback_case_update` (a reply or a state change; never the content), `review_request_update`, `review_scope_sent` (never the price), `review_booked` (both participants, with a calendar attachment), `review_report_shared`. ## 13. Known gaps and follow-ups

Owner decisions, in the order they matter:

1. **Self-access after membership end (D-EL-07, PRI-05).** The engine refuses a personal context without an ACTIVE membership, so a former employee cannot read their own feedback cases or receipt. Allowing it is an authorization-strategy change.
2. **Org-unit-scoped reporting grants** cannot read the organization-level reports: the report resource is the organization. A team manager with a department-scoped `reporting_user` grant sees the permission block. Either the reports gain a cohort-scoped resource or managers receive organization-scoped reporting grants.
3. **Production configuration is entered by its owners (spec 16):** real recipient process and sensitive-route policy, service scope and prices, approval ceilings, retention references. The catalogue ships with "Request a quote" and one explicitly free call; nothing is invented.
4. **Payments** stay outside the platform (D-EL-04); accepting a scope records authority and NestSelf invoices under the accepted terms.
5. **Scheduling** depends on the coordinator proposing windows (D-EL-03) until an availability service exists.
6. **Notifications are emails only** (D-EL-05); in-app notifications and digest deduplication across modules are not built (OPS-01 is met by one mail per event with neutral bodies).
7. **Attachments (FBK-07) and custom or lifecycle surveys (SUR-05)** are out of the pilot, as the spec allows.
8. **Changing a route's handlers does not reassign open cases**; they keep their handler until reassigned by hand.
9. **The listening jobs are not in `run-job.ts`**; they run through the worker or `ListeningJobsService.run` in tests.
10. **The reviewer's admin screens assume the expert context** and switch into it once; a staff member who is also a reviewer switches contexts through the header picker.
11. **The heatmap row-header check at 390 px in the Playwright journey runs only when a result is published**; against the reseeded e2e database it verifies the empty state and the absence of horizontal scrolling, and the row header geometry is covered by the workspace unit tests.
12. **Mobile (Expo)** has no listening screens; the invitation mail links to the web survey.
