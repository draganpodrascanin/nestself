The plan in brief

What we build and the loop it closes

Three formats on one real-time layer. Epic NS-CALLS-01, milestones M0 to M4.

Private one-to-one consultations; webinars where employees watch one or two presenters without seeing each other; small workshops on video. All three share the Chime adapter, the session model, the client, the event ingestion and the diagnostics.

A consultation

  1. Neutral reminder

    "Your NestSelf session", 24 h and 1 h before, private calendar entry.

  2. Pre-join check

    Devices and network, camera off if wanted, blur, permission help.

  3. Private room

    Only the two participants, 15 min before to 10 min after; not recorded.

  4. Outcome, then follow-up

    The expert records the outcome and proposes a follow-up; the employee confirms or suggests a time; a manager approves if the company policy says so.

  5. Private feedback

    Optional, one to five, never visible to the employer.

A webinar

  1. Discover and register

    Eligible employees register; capacity enforced; "this webinar is recorded" stated up front.

  2. Lobby

    Title, presenter, countdown; "your camera stays off; write to the hosts or raise your hand".

  3. Live

    Presenter video and slides; no roster, no attendee cameras; chat to the hosts, published anonymously; polls; the floor (audio) given by a host.

  4. Replay

    Presenter-only recording of every webinar, reviewed as content before anyone sees it.

In this epic

  • A minimal appointment spine; the Chime adapter with a local fake
  • Pre-join and in-call client; screens in both web apps
  • Follow-up after a call, with manager approval by company policy
  • Webinars: registration, reminders, chat, polls, the floor, moderation
  • Presenter-only recording of every webinar, handoff to the CMS
  • Diagnostics, audit, neutral emails, tests, infrastructure
  • M3: audiences up to 1,000, certificates; M4: mobile, workshops, captions

Deliberately not in it

  • Availability, booking, funding, rescheduling rules (a booking epic)
  • Review and publication of replays (the CMS epic)
  • Recording of consultations (not for now); attendee cameras (never)
  • Extending a running session (replaced by the follow-up)
  • Live captions; phone dial-in; SMS and push; professional notes
  • Native mobile call modules, screen share from phones
  • A Hungarian launch (the locale stays for Vojvodina)

The plan in brief

Risks, and the legal checklist

Serbian law only. Checklist items are launch conditions for the production account and never block development.

Risks we accept knowingly

  • No maintained React Native module. Mobile uses the web client inside the app; screen share from phones is not possible.
  • Serbian and Hungarian live captions. Not supported by Chime transcription; replays are captioned later with review.
  • Recording pipeline quota. Ten concurrent pipelines by default in Frankfurt; a quota request before the first busy month.
  • Corporate networks. Blocked UDP falls back to TCP 443 with lower quality; client companies get a one-page network note.

Risks we watch

  • Parallel auth work. Both efforts edit the action and template catalogue; those tasks go last and rebase first.
  • Undocumented idempotency. Chime does not document what a repeated create request returns; the Redis lock is the real protection, measured in spike 1.
  • The floor in replicated meetings. A reported audio dropout after 40 minutes; measured at the start of M3 before replication is built.
  • Approval policy without an approver. A company that requires approval but names no approver for a unit: the proposal fails visibly and tells the employee to ask HR.
  • Recorded voices. An attendee given the floor is on the recording; the notice before unmuting is logged, since Serbian law on recording is explicit about consent.

Before the first real consultation in production

  1. DPIA under ZZPL Art.

  2. Webinar recording notice: registration, confirmation email and lobby state that the webinar is recorded and that only presenters are on the recording; an attendee given the floor is told their voice is recorded (D-CW-34; LAW-10 on Art.

  3. Product copy: "encrypted in transit and at rest", never "end-to-end encrypted" (LAW-07); the accessibility statement on captions (D-CW-23).

  4. Expert credentials and confidentiality under Serbian law: psychologists' professional secrecy (LAW-15); the expert onboarding epic captures the licence; the Commissioner's position that an employer must never learn a diagnosis (LAW-14) is why the approval item of D-CW-37 carries no category.

  5. Client-company network note: *.chime.aws on UDP 3478 and TCP 443, *.sdkassets.chime.aws on TCP 443 (CORE-30).

  6. Service quota requests in eu-central-1 before scale (D-CW-31).

  7. Volume pricing conversation with AWS after 1,000 consultations per month (COST-10).

  8. Kept for a later Hungarian launch, not for now: Hungarian recording consent and telemedicine rules, psychotherapy licensing, NAIH practice (LAW-19 to LAW-25); Hungarian crisis hotlines in the crisis block.

D-CW-01
One epic, four milestones ruled, default applies
M0 foundation, M1 consultations with the end-of-call follow-up, M2 webinars live and always recorded, M3 scale and certificates, M4 mobile, workshops and captions. The formats share the adapter, the session model, the client and the diagnostics.
D-CW-02
A minimal appointment spine, not the booking engine ruled, default applies
Nothing about appointments exists in the code. The epic adds only what a call needs. Availability, booking, funding and rescheduling are a later epic behind a fixed contract seam.
D-CW-03
Webinar occurrences here, replays in the CMS ruled, default applies
Registration, live session, Q&A and the recording up to a finished MP4 are built here. Review and publication of the replay follow the CMS epic's workflow.
D-CW-04
Out of scope for the whole epic ruled, default applies
Phone dial-in and SMS, professional notes, settlement, employer reports, live captions, webinars above 1,000 attendees, native mobile modules and chat in consultations.
D-CW-05
A provider adapter with a fake for local work settled
A VideoProvider with the Chime implementation and a deterministic fake for development and tests. Only the current chime-sdk-meetings namespace is used.
D-CW-06
Everything in eu-central-1 settled
Control, media and pipeline regions are hard-coded and validated at start-up. Product copy says encrypted in transit and at rest, never end-to-end, because audio is mixed on the server.
D-CW-07
The meeting is created at the first join ruled, spike pending
A Chime meeting ends after five empty minutes, so it cannot be created at booking. A Redis lock serialises concurrent first joins; the idempotency token is a second line of defence.
D-CW-08
Rejoin reuses the same attendee ruled, default applies
Join tokens have no clock expiry. Credentials are cached in Redis for the window, never written to PostgreSQL or logs, and the response is marked no-store.
D-CW-09
Explicit end plus a safety net ruled, default applies
End for everyone deletes the meeting at once. A delayed job closes any room at the scheduled end plus 15 minutes. No extension: the follow-up is scheduled on the post-call screens instead.
D-CW-10
Meeting features per format ruled, default applies
720p video, echo reduction on, full HD content share. Capacity 4 for a consultation, 250 for a webinar, 30 for a workshop. Webinar attendees are receive-only.
D-CW-11
Opaque identities towards the provider ruled, default applies
Random per-session participant ids as external ids and the organisation id as tenant id. No name or email ever leaves the platform.
D-CW-12
Provider events through a queue into the worker ruled, default applies
Direct queue notifications plus an EventBridge backstop. Every event is stored once under a unique key and applied safely out of order; attendee-minutes are derived from events.
D-CW-13
Client observability without content ruled, default applies
The browser posts failure events and 30-second quality aggregates to the API. Kept 90 days. Alerts on join failures and slow starts.
D-CW-14
Webinar delivery by audience size ruled, default applies
One meeting up to 250 attendees in M2. Media replication to 1,000 in M3, because 200 to 300 attendees are expected in the first year. Streaming beyond that is documented, not built.
D-CW-15
Attendee anonymity enforced on the server ruled, default applies
Attendees never send video. No roster for attendees, a count for presenters, names only for the moderator. A host gives the floor, audio only; the attendee unmutes to speak; the host can take it back.
D-CW-16
Interaction on Socket.IO and PostgreSQL ruled, default applies
The webinar chat, hand-raise, polls and quick signals use the existing gateway with authorised rooms. Chime data messages and Chime messaging are not used.
D-CW-17
Audience policy, registration and windows ruled, default applies
All eligible members, chosen organisations or units. Capacity is locked at registration; the recording is announced there. Hosts join 30 minutes before, attendees 15. Employers never receive lists.
D-CW-18

Stories left out on purpose

  • Attendee video in webinars. Attendees never get a camera; the floor gives audio only. This is a permanent constraint, not a deferral. (D-CW-35)
