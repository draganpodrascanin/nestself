## Defects found while capturing

- **DEF-1**: amazon-chime-sdk-js reads Node's `global` (CSPMonitor.register in DefaultMeetingSession); browsers have none. Every pre-join check failed (camera and microphone "Not working", connection "Offline") and Join stayed disabled, in both apps. Fixed by ee2dc6b fix(video): define global before loading the Chime SDK (T-CW-1.4); the screens were recaptured after the fix.
- **DEF-2**: The workspace and admin Tailwind builds did not scan packages/video/src. Classes used only by the call components were missing: the self-view covered the whole stage, overlays lost their stacking, the preview box its aspect ratio. Fixed by 298316a fix(web): scan the video package for Tailwind classes in both apps; the screens were recaptured after the fix.
- **DEF-3**: GET /auth/me returned no effective actions for the expert: assignment-scoped grants (assigned_cases) never passed the synthetic-resource check. The admin app showed "This view is not available to your role" on /consultations and /consultations/:id, so the expert could not join, end, record an outcome or propose a follow-up. Fixed by 88180b7 fix(api): effective actions include assignment-scoped grants backed by an active assignment (T-CW-1.1); the screens were recaptured after the fix.
