This manual explains the video consultations and webinars of NestSelf as each person meets them: what you see, what each button does, and what NestSelf keeps private. Read the chapter for your role; the last three chapters are for everyone.

Employees

You use the workspace app. Chapter 2 follows a consultation from the appointment list to the follow-up; chapter 5 covers webinars.

Experts

You use the admin app. Chapter 3 covers your consultations, the outcome and the follow-up; chapter 6 covers presenting a webinar.

HR and approvers

You use the workspace app, in the employer space. Chapter 4 covers the follow-up approval policy and deciding requests.

NestSelf operations and support

You use the admin app. Chapter 6 covers creating and moderating webinars; chapter 7 covers booking consultations and call diagnostics.

About the screenshots

Every screenshot comes from the sample environment: both web apps ran against the NestSelf API with a test database of fictional sample people, and a stand-in replaced the video service. The other person's video is a drawing labelled “Sample video”, and the camera preview is the browser's test pattern. The people are fictional: Jovan Petrović (employee), Milica Ristić (expert), Ivana Jovanović (HR and approver) and Nikola Đorđević (operations and webinar moderator). No real personal data appears.

Each screen is shown on a computer (1280 by 800, left) and on a phone (390 by 844, right). The screen numbers match the files in manual/screenshots/. Lists in the screenshots may hold several similar entries that the test runs created.

How the text is written

Buttons, fields and menu entries are in bold green, for example Join session. Messages the app shows are in quotes, for example “Five minutes left.” Both are copied from the app's English wording.

Times are always shown in the time zone of the appointment or webinar, Europe/Belgrade in the screenshots. Where a message contains a time or a date, this manual writes HH:MM or DATE.

The product ships in Serbian (Latin), Serbian (Cyrillic) and Hungarian as well as English, and every screen works the same in each language. A Serbian edition of this manual is the same build with the sr-Latn locale.

You meet a NestSelf expert in a private video session in the workspace app. Only the two of you are in the room, and the session is not recorded. This chapter follows one consultation from the appointment list to the follow-up.

2.1

Your appointments

The appointment list

Open Appointments in the menu. Upcoming lists the sessions to come, the earliest first; Past lists the ones that are over. Each row shows the date and time, the service and your expert's first name. A badge shows the status: Confirmed, Waiting for approval, Completed, Canceled, Declined, Missed, or Follow-up proposed when your expert proposed another session. Select a row to open it.

Before each session you receive an email 24 hours and 1 hour ahead. Its subject is always “Your NestSelf session” and its calendar entry is private, so nothing on a lock screen says what the session is about. If you are in the app at that moment, a short notice says “Reminder: your NestSelf session starts at HH:MM.”

2.2

Before the session: the join window

Joining opens 15 minutes before the start. The first join is possible until 10 minutes after the start; if you already joined and dropped out, you can rejoin until the room closes, 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

Before the window opens

The appointment page shows your expert, the date, the time, the service and the status. Until the window opens, the button reads Come back at HH:MM and cannot be selected, with the hint “You can join from 15 minutes before the start.”

You can cancel here. Cancel appointment asks “Cancel this appointment?” first, and a canceled appointment cannot be restored.

When the window is open

From 15 minutes before the start, Join session is active. Select it to check your devices. If you open the page after the start, it says “Your session started at HH:MM. You can still join for the remaining time.” The timer then counts only the time that remains.

If you already joined and lost the call, the page offers Rejoin session and says “If you already joined this session, you can rejoin until HH:MM.”

When the window has closed

If you come too late for the first join, the page says “The join window for this session has closed.” and shows a link to technical support instead of the button. You also receive an email asking whether a technical problem kept you away, with a link to report it.

If the server refuses the join

The server checks the window again when you select Join now. If it has closed in the meantime, the device check shows “This session cannot be joined right now. Joining opens shortly before the start and closes 10 minutes after it; if you already joined, you can rejoin until shortly after the end.”

2.3

Checking your devices

Every call starts with a short device check. You join only when your microphone works.

The device check

The page “Check your camera and microphone” checks your Camera, Microphone, Speaker and Connection. Each shows Working, Blocked by the browser or Not working. Speak and watch the microphone bar move; select Play test sound and answer “Did you hear the test sound?” Choose another camera, microphone or speaker from the three lists if needed. The connection reads Good, Fair, Poor or Offline.

If the browser blocks the camera or microphone, a box titled “Allow your camera and microphone” explains what to do in your browser; then select Check again. Join now stays disabled until the microphone is allowed.

For a psychological consultation the page first offers an optional field, Your current location: “Share your current location with the expert for safety purposes.” “Only your expert sees it, and only during this session. It is deleted when the session ends. You can leave it empty.” It sits above the check, outside the screenshot.

Camera off and background blur

Switch on Join with camera off to join without video; the preview shows “Your camera is off” and the hint “You can turn your camera on during the call.” Switch on Blur my background to blur the room behind you: “Blur hides most of the room behind you, but not all of it. A private space is still best.” Both choices are optional and are remembered on this device for your next session. Some browsers cannot blur: “Background blur is not available in this browser.”

2.4

Waiting for your expert

The waiting card

After Join now you are in the call. If your expert has not joined yet, the stage says “Your expert will join shortly” and a card shows “Milica will join shortly”, the scheduled time and three tips under “While you wait”.

If your expert is running late, the card says so: “Your expert is running late and will join in about 5 minutes.” or “Your expert is running a few minutes late.” You do not need to do anything; the call starts when the expert joins.

2.5

During the call

The call screen

Your expert fills the screen and your own video sits in a corner. Move it with “Move your video to another corner” or hide it; hidden, it says “Your video is hidden from you. The other participant still sees it.” At the top: the timer with time elapsed and time left, the connection badge, and Crisis resources.

The control bar: Mute, Turn camera off, Blur background, Devices, Full screen, I cannot hear you, I cannot see you and Leave. Every control works with the keyboard. A consultation has no chat and is never recorded, so no recording control or sign appears.

On a phone, and the quick signals

On a phone the video fills the screen and the bar keeps the main controls. More opens the rest: blur, full screen, I cannot hear you and I cannot see you.

I cannot hear you sends a short notice instead of a chat message. The other person sees “The other participant cannot hear you.” The notice is not kept after the session. I cannot see you works the same way.

2.6

Your connection

The badge shows Strong connection, Unstable connection or Poor connection, measured every 30 seconds. On a weak connection the app protects the sound: it lowers your video first, then pauses it.

An unstable connection

When the connection weakens, the badge turns yellow and a banner says “Your connection is unstable. Video quality may drop for a moment.” If it gets worse: “Your connection is poor. Video quality may be lowered to keep the sound clear.” You can keep talking.

Video paused, audio continues

After two poor measurements your outgoing video pauses and the banner says “Your video is paused due to connection quality. Audio continues.” When the connection recovers, your video comes back by itself and the banner disappears.

Connection lost

If the connection drops, the app shows “Connection lost. Reconnecting…” and tries for up to two minutes. If that fails, it shows “Unable to reconnect” and “The connection could not be restored. Rejoin to continue.” with Rejoin and Contact support. Rejoining puts you back into the same session while the room is open.

2.7

Crisis resources

Help is one tap away

Crisis resources stays at the top of the screen for the whole call. It opens “Need urgent help?” with “If you or someone else is in immediate danger, call the emergency number now.” The list is for Serbia: Emergency number 112, Ambulance 194, and Centar Srce, emotional support 0800 300 303. On a phone, each number can be called directly. The panel ends with “NestSelf is not an emergency service. In an emergency, call 112.”

Opening the panel does not leave or mute the call. Close it with the cross.

2.8

Time remaining and leaving

The five-minute cue

The timer shows the time elapsed and the time left. Five minutes before the end, “Five minutes left.” appears; dismiss it with the cross. At the scheduled end the timer says “Session time has ended.” Ten minutes later: “The room will close in 5 minutes.” The room closes 15 minutes after the scheduled end and the call stops for both of you.

When the session ends

When your expert ends the session, the stage says “The consultation has ended.” for five seconds, then the post-call screen opens. You can leave at any time with Leave; this does not end the session for your expert, and your screen then says “You left the consultation”.

2.9

After the call

Private feedback

The post-call screen asks “How was your session?” Choose a rating from 1 to 5 and add a comment if you like. The screen says “Your feedback is private and your employer will not see it.” Select Send feedback, or Skip to send nothing. NestSelf reads feedback only in aggregate.

Reporting a technical problem

If something went wrong, select Report a technical problem. Choose what went wrong (Sound, Video, Connection or Something else). Under “Details that are sent” the dialog lists exactly what is sent: call length, reconnections, connection quality, browser and operating system. “We send the connection details of this call to NestSelf support. Nothing you said, no names and no device names are included.”

After Send report: “Report sent. NestSelf support can now see the connection details of this call.”

2.10

Follow-up proposals

After the call your expert can propose another session. It reaches you on the post-call screen, in an email titled “A follow-up was proposed” and on the appointment page and list, so you do not miss it if you already left. You decide.

A proposal arrives

The card “Your expert proposed a follow-up” shows the date, the time and the length, and says “You decide: confirm this time, suggest other times or decline.” Choose Confirm, Suggest other times or Decline.

Suggesting other times

Suggest other times opens “Suggest up to three times that suit you”. Fill in a date with a start and an end (From and To); Add another time adds a second and a third option. Times are in the appointment's time zone. Select Send suggestions. A time in the past is refused with “Choose a time in the future.”

After you suggested times

The card says “You suggested other times. The expert will choose one of them.” The status stays Follow-up proposed until the expert picks one; the session then starts at the beginning of the time chosen and keeps its length. After that it is booked, or it waits for approval, as below.

Confirming

What Confirm does depends on your organisation. If follow-ups need no approval, the session is booked at once: “Your follow-up is booked for DATE at HH:MM.” Reminders follow as for any appointment. If your organisation approves follow-ups first, the card shows “Waiting for approval” and “Your organisation approves follow-ups before they are booked. You will both be told when it is decided.”

What “Waiting for approval” means

Some organisations ask an approver to agree before a follow-up is booked. The approver sees only your name, that it is a follow-up session with a NestSelf expert, the date and the length. They never see the service, the topic, your expert or anything said in the session.

  • If nobody is assigned to approve for your team, confirming fails with “Your organisation has not assigned anyone to approve follow-ups yet. Contact your HR team.”
  • If the approver does not decide in time, the request lapses: “The approval lapsed before the proposed time.”
  • If the proposed time passes before you answer: “The proposed time has passed. Your expert can propose a new one.”

Waiting for the decision

The appointment page shows the status Waiting for approval and the same sentence. You and your expert are both told when the approver decides.

Approved

An approved follow-up is an ordinary Confirmed appointment: you join it like the first one, from 15 minutes before the start.

Declined by the approver

If the approver declines, the page shows their short reason: “Your manager declined this follow-up: Please book it after the team planning week.” Only you see the reason. Talk to your expert or HR if you still need the session.

Declining yourself

Decline ends the proposal; the card says “You declined this follow-up.” Your expert is told and can propose another time.

2.11

What your employer can and cannot see

Your employer cannot see

  • that you booked, joined or missed a consultation;
  • its service or topic, its outcome or its length;
  • your feedback, your technical reports or the location you shared;
  • which webinars you registered for or attended, or what you wrote in them.

This applies to every employer account: owners, HR, managers and reporting users. The server refuses these records to them on every route; hiding a button is not what protects you.

What your employer can see

  • If your organisation requires approval, the approver sees your name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and the length, and nothing else.
  • Any number about calls or webinars shown to an employer in the future must count at least five people. No such number exists yet.
  • Emails and calendar entries are neutral: “Your NestSelf session” or “Your NestSelf webinar”, with a private calendar entry.

You run consultations from the NestSelf admin app: you see your list, tell the employee if you are late, join, share your screen, end the session, record what happened and propose a follow-up. You see only the consultations you are assigned to.

Your consultations

Open Consultations. Upcoming comes first, then Past; you see only the consultations you are assigned to. The columns are Employee (first name only), Service, Time in each appointment's time zone, Status and Outcome: Not yet, To record, Delivered, No-show or Not delivered. Select the employee's name to open a consultation.

Before you join: running late

The consultation page lists the participants, the date, the time, the outcome and the status. Inside the join window it offers Join session and, if you are delayed, Estimated delay with Send running late. The employee sees the delay in the waiting card, and you see “The employee was told that you are running late.”

Join session opens the same device check as the employee's: camera, microphone, speaker and connection, with camera off and blur. Joining is possible from 15 minutes before the start.

Your call screen

Your call has the employee's controls plus two of your own: Share screen and End for everyone. While you share, you see “You are sharing your screen” and the employee sees your screen in the main view; Stop sharing ends it. Only you can share.

If the employee shared a location, the card “Location the employee shared” shows it, with “Visible to you only during this session. It is deleted when the session ends.” Otherwise it says “The employee has not shared a location.” Your Crisis resources adds “If the employee may be in danger”: stay in the call, ask where they are, and call 112 from another phone. Your five-minute cue adds “You can propose a follow-up on the screen after the call.”

Ending for everyone

End for everyone asks “End the consultation for both of you?” “The call closes for you and the employee. You record the outcome on the next screen.” Choose Keep talking or End consultation. The call closes for both of you, and the outcome form opens.

Recording the outcome

“Record the session outcome” asks for one choice: Completed, The employee did not join, I could not join, Technical failure or Canceled. Select Save outcome. If you closed the form, Record the outcome on the page opens it again.

There is no notes field, by design: “Choose what happened. No notes are recorded.” NestSelf does not store professional notes, and the server refuses a note if one is sent. Only the delivery status and the connection data are kept.

Proposing a follow-up

Once the outcome is Completed, the card “Propose a follow-up” appears. Pick the Date, the Time and the Duration (the same as this session unless you change it); times are in the appointment's time zone. The card says “The employee decides whether this time works. They can confirm it, suggest other times or decline.” There is no free text. Select Send proposal.

The card then shows “Waiting for the employee to answer.” If the organisation approves follow-ups, you see “The employee's organisation approves follow-ups before they are booked. You will both be told when it is decided.” If the employee declines, a notice says “The employee declined your follow-up proposal.” and the card offers a new proposal.

Picking a time the employee suggested

If the employee suggests other times, open the follow-up with Open the follow-up. The page says “The employee suggested other times” and “Choose one of them. The session starts at the beginning of the time you choose and keeps its length.” Choose one under Suggested times and select Schedule; the page then says “Scheduled for DATE at HH:MM.” From there the follow-up is booked or goes to approval, as when the employee confirms. This edition has no screenshot of this screen.

A follow-up is refused when the session was not completed, another follow-up is already open or the time has passed: “A follow-up is not possible here: the session was not completed, another follow-up is already open, or the proposed time has passed.”

An organisation can ask for approval before a follow-up session is booked. HR sets the policy once; the approver for the employee's team then decides each request, seeing four facts and nothing about the content.

The follow-up approval policy

HR administrators open Follow-up approval in the employer space. “When an expert proposes a follow-up session and the employee confirms it, choose whether it is booked straight away or needs an approver first.” Two choices:

  • Book without approval: “The session is booked as soon as the employee confirms it. Nobody else is asked.” This is the default.
  • An approver decides first: “The approver for the employee’s team decides before the session is booked. They see only the employee’s name, that it is a follow-up session with a NestSelf expert, the date and the length, never the topic or the expert. If no approver covers the employee’s team, the follow-up cannot be confirmed until you name one.”

Select Save; the app confirms “The follow-up approval policy is saved.”

The approvals list

Approvers open Approvals. “Approve or decline follow-up sessions for people in your team. You see only who it is for, when it is and how long it lasts.” Under Waiting for your decision each request shows four facts: the employee's name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and time with the time zone, and the length. Select Approve or Decline. An approval tells both sides: “Follow-up approved. Jovan Petrović and the expert are told.”

Earlier requests move to Decided with Approved, Not approved or Expired.

Declining with a reason

Decline opens “Decline this follow-up?” with “Jovan Petrović will see your reason. Keep it short and leave out personal or health details.” The Reason is required: “Up to 300 characters. Only the employee sees it.” An empty reason shows “Write a short reason.” After Decline: “Follow-up declined. Jovan Petrović will see your reason.”

What an approver sees, and never sees

  • Sees: the employee's name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and time, the length.
  • Never sees: the service or topic, the expert's name or specialty, the outcome, feedback or anything said in a session.
  • Nobody decides their own follow-up: “You cannot decide your own follow-up. Your alternate approver can decide it.”
  • The alternate approver receives the same emails and can decide too.

When nobody decides: lapse rules

  • The request email goes to the approver and the alternate.
  • After 5 working days without a decision, both receive a reminder. Working days are Monday to Friday in Belgrade time; public holidays are not taken into account.
  • The request lapses at the earlier of the proposed start and 10 working days after the request. It becomes Expired, the follow-up is declined, and the employee is told.
  • A decision after the lapse is refused: “This request has already been decided or has expired.”
  • No reminder is sent when it would arrive after the lapse.

Webinars are live talks by NestSelf experts for employees of client companies. You watch and listen and are never on camera. Every webinar is recorded, and only the presenters are on the recording; your voice is recorded only if a host gives you the floor and you unmute.

5.1

Finding and registering

Webinars open to you

Open Webinars: “Live sessions open to you. Register to get a place.” You see only the webinars your organisation's audience includes. Each shows the title, the date and time with the time zone, the presenters, Upcoming, the places left, and beside Register the notice “This webinar is recorded; only the presenters are on the recording.”

The webinar page

The page repeats the recording notice and adds “Live captions are not yet available. Replays are captioned after review.” Under Registration it says “You are not registered yet.” Select Register. A full webinar says “This webinar is full.”

You receive a confirmation email titled “Your NestSelf webinar”; it repeats that the webinar is recorded and only the presenters are on the recording, and adds a private calendar entry. Reminders follow 24 hours and 1 hour before.

Registered

The page says “You are registered for this webinar.” Cancel registration gives your place back until the webinar starts. Under Joining the webinar: “Your camera stays off; you can write to the hosts and raise your hand to ask for the floor.”, a countdown, and Join webinar, which opens 15 minutes before the start (“You can join from HH:MM.”).

If a webinar is canceled

Registered employees receive a cancellation email, and the webinar page says “This webinar was canceled.” There is nothing to join, and the webinar cannot be registered for again.

5.2

The lobby and the live webinar

The lobby

Join webinar first asks you to “Check your sound”: “Attendees listen and watch. Your camera stays off, and your microphone stays off unless a host gives you the floor.” Then the lobby says “The hosts have not started yet. You join automatically when they start.” The card shows the title, the presenters, the start and a countdown, the recording notice, “Your camera stays off; you can write to the hosts and raise your hand to ask for the floor.” and “If a host gives you the floor, your voice is part of the recording.”

Nobody sees who else is waiting, and you can already write to the hosts.

The live stage

You see the presenter's video and any shared screen, with Live and REC. Your controls: Mute the webinar sound, Raise hand, Full screen and Leave. There is no camera button and no list of who else is there. When you leave, nobody else is told.

Chat and questions

In Chat, choose Message or Question under “What are you sending?”, write under Write to the hosts (up to 1,000 characters) and select Send to the hosts. “Your message was sent to the hosts. It appears in the chat only if a host publishes it.”

Your message is anonymous to the audience by default. Tick Show my first name with this message to add your first name: “Without your first name, the audience does not see who wrote it.” The moderator always sees your registered name. Published messages show as Participant or your first name; hosts' messages as Host; answers under “Answer from the hosts”.

Polls

When the moderator opens a poll, the Poll panel shows the question and the options. Choose one and select Vote: “Thank you, your vote was counted. The results appear when the poll closes.” “Votes are counted without names.” When the poll closes, the results appear as percentages.

5.3

Asking for the floor

Raising your hand

Raise hand tells the hosts you would like to speak: “Your hand is raised. A host can give you the floor.” Lower hand takes it back. The moderator sees your name; the presenter sees only “A participant asked to speak”.

Being given the floor

If a host gives you the floor, a dialog opens: “You have the floor”, “You can speak now, unmute to talk.” and “Your voice is part of the recording. Your camera stays off.” Your microphone stays off until you choose Unmute now; Not now keeps it off. Others see “A participant is speaking”. Your camera never turns on.

Muted by a host, and asked to unmute

While you have the floor, a host can mute you: “A host muted your microphone. You can speak again when a host asks you to unmute.” A host can then ask you to speak again. The question “The host asks you to unmute. Unmute?” offers Unmute and Stay muted; nobody can unmute you but you. When the host takes the floor back: “The host took the floor back. Your microphone is off again.”

The floor in a large webinar

Above 250 attendees, the audience is spread over linked meetings that receive only the presenters. When a host gives the floor to an attendee in one of them, the app joins that attendee's microphone to the presenters' meeting for the turn, sound only, and takes it out again when a host mutes them or takes the floor back. The dialog and the controls are the same as above. Whether the sound stays stable in long turns is not measured yet (chapter 9).

After the webinar

When the hosts end the webinar you see “Thank you for joining”, “The webinar has ended.” and “A replay will be available after review.” The recording goes to NestSelf's content team for review before anyone can watch it.

Webinars are created, published and run in the admin app. Operations prepare the occurrence; the presenters speak; the moderator reviews messages, gives the floor, runs polls and can stop the recording.

6.1

Creating and publishing a webinar

Operations create and publish webinars. Presenters are NestSelf experts; the moderator, from NestSelf operations, runs the room.

The webinar list

Open Webinars: “Create, publish and run webinars.” The list shows Title, Date and time, Status (Draft, Upcoming, Live now, Ended or Canceled) and Capacity. Select New webinar to create one.

A new webinar

Fill in the Title in at least one language, an optional Description, and the Audience: Every eligible organisation, Selected organisations or Units of one organisation. Capacity takes “From 1 to 1,000 attendees.” Tick the Presenters and choose the Moderator: “Reviews messages, gives the floor and can stop the recording. Publishing needs a moderator.” Set the date, start and end in Europe/Belgrade time.

There is no recording switch: the form states that the webinar is recorded. Select Save draft: “The draft is saved. Publish it when it is ready.”

How many people can attend

Up to 250 attendees share one meeting. For a larger audience, up to 1,000, the platform adds linked audience meetings that receive only the presenters; attendees see no difference, and the chat, polls and attendance work across them. Each attendee's place is reserved when they register.

Publishing, editing and canceling

The draft page lists the facts. Publish asks “Publish this webinar?” “Eligible employees can see it and register.” Publishing needs: “Before publishing, add at least one presenter and a moderator, set a start in the future and choose an audience.”

Publishing makes the webinar visible to the eligible employees of its audience, who can then register: “The webinar is published.”

Canceling a webinar

Cancel webinar asks “Cancel this webinar?” “Registered employees receive a cancellation email. This cannot be undone.” Choose Keep the webinar to go back, or confirm: “The webinar is canceled.” Reminders stop, and registering is no longer possible.

Editing a webinar

Edit opens “Edit webinar”, the same form filled in, while the webinar is a draft or upcoming. “The audience can be changed only while the webinar is a draft.” Select Save changes; the page confirms “Changes saved.” A webinar that is live, ended or canceled cannot be changed: “This webinar can no longer be changed in its current status.”

6.2

Running the webinar

The presenter view

Hosts open Open the live view up to 30 minutes before the start. “Check your setup” runs the device check with an optional Test screen sharing and shows “Registered attendees: N”, a number and never names. In the lobby, Start webinar starts the webinar: “Attendees wait in the lobby until you start. The recording starts with the webinar.”

The presenter sees Attending: N, Raised hands: N and Published messages: N, shares the screen with Share screen, and writes Write to everyone with Send to everyone; host messages appear at once. A presenter sees only published messages and, for raised hands, “A participant asked to speak” with a count.

The moderator console

The Moderation panel shows the attendance count, the recording badge and Stop recording. Under Raised hands each hand shows the registered name, the time it was raised and Give the floor.

Waiting for review lists each message with its author and “Anonymous to the audience” or “First name shown”: Publish, Edit (then Publish edited text), Answer (then Publish answer) or Dismiss. A dismissed message never reaches the audience.

When an attendee has the floor

The attendee appears under Speaking now. You can Mute them; a muted speaker shows Muted and Ask to unmute, which sends the request and then reads Asked to unmute. Only the attendee can unmute. Take the floor back ends their turn and turns their microphone off.

Polls and ending the webinar

Under Prepare a poll, write a question and two to six options and select Add poll: “Poll added. Open it when you are ready.” Open poll shows it to everyone; Close poll publishes the results without names.

End webinar for everyone asks “End this webinar?” “All attendees will be disconnected.” Choose Keep the webinar going or end it. Presenters and the moderator can both end the webinar; otherwise the room closes 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

Stopping the recording early

The recording starts by itself with the webinar, shows REC to everyone and stops when the webinar ends. Only the moderator can stop it earlier: “Stop the recording now?” “The rest of the webinar is not recorded, and the recording cannot be started again.” Choose Keep recording or Stop recording. If the recording fails, everyone sees “The recording failed. The webinar continues without a recording.”

Attendance after the webinar

Once the webinar ran, its page shows Attendance: Registered: N, Joined: N and Most at the same time: N, then a table titled “Attendance per registration, without names”: one row per registration (Registration N) with Joined, Left, Minutes and Completed. Only moderators and NestSelf operations can open it, every view is recorded, and employers never receive it. The page also shows the Recording state.

Recording states on the webinar page

  • Starts with the webinar: before the start.
  • Recording now: while live.
  • Being processed: the pieces are joined into one video file.
  • Ready: the file exists, in Frankfurt, encrypted at rest.
  • Handed to the content team: passed to the content team for review.
  • Failed: no recording; the webinar went on without one.

What “handed off” means

The replay is never published straight from the meeting. The content team reviews it first and decides whether and when attendees can watch it. Today the hand-off is noted as pending: the content system's media interface does not exist yet, so the content team collects the file from its folder (see chapter 9).

Only presenters are on the recording: the video, the sound and the shared screen of the presenters, and the voice of an attendee who was given the floor and unmuted.

NestSelf operations book consultations for client companies and keep an overview; support operators look into technical problems from connection data, never from content. This edition has no screenshots of these screens.

Booking a consultation

Client operations open Consultations and select New consultation: “Book a consultation for an employee of an organisation you support, with an expert approved for the service.” Choose the Organisation, the Employee, the Service and the Expert, then the date, Start time and End time. Times are in Europe/Belgrade. Select Book consultation: “Consultation booked for DATE at HH:MM.”

The form refuses an expert who is not approved for the service (“This expert is not approved for this service.”) and an employee from another organisation (“This employee is not a member of the organisation.”). Reminders are scheduled at once.

The operations list

Consultations lists every consultation of the organisations you coordinate, with Expert and Organisation columns. Filters: Organisation, Status, From and To, with Apply filters and Clear filters. View opens a read-only page: “Operations view: read-only. Only the assigned expert joins the call or records the outcome.”

Canceling a consultation

Cancel appointment asks “Cancel this appointment?” and “DATE at HH:MM. Both participants are told, and it cannot be undone.” Choose Keep appointment or Cancel appointment. Only a confirmed appointment can be canceled. Both participants receive an email, and a call that is already running closes. The employee can cancel their own appointment; the expert cannot cancel.

Call support

Support operators open Call support: “Look up a call session to see its connection diagnostics. Every lookup is recorded.” Enter the Session ID and select Look up. The page shows the session's status, provider, start, end and meeting id, and for each participant the role, join and leave times, reconnections, connection quality, error codes, browser and operating system. It never shows names, content or device names.

Technical problem reports lists the reports employees and experts sent, with Problem and the connection summary; Diagnostics opens the session. Listing every report needs platform-wide access; otherwise: “Listing problem reports needs platform-wide diagnostics access. Look up a session by its ID above instead.”

Sample mode

When an app runs on sample data, every screen shows the banner “Sample data. Nothing here is real, saved or sent.” and no action sends or saves anything. The call and webinar screens can be tried with Try the call screen with sample data, which says “This is a demonstration with sample data. No call is started, and nothing is recorded or sent.”

Approvals in sample mode show fictional requests and say “Sample mode: these requests are fictional. Approving or declining sends nothing.” or, with nothing to show, “Sample mode: there is nothing to decide here and nothing is sent.” The policy screen says “Sample mode: your choice is not saved.”

Who can do what today

  • Book and cancel consultations: client operations, for the organisations they coordinate.
  • Create, publish, edit and cancel webinars: holders of webinar management (operations, content publishers).
  • Moderate webinars: client operations and the platform owner.
  • Look up call diagnostics: support operators.
  • Service coordinators cannot yet moderate, book or cancel (chapter 9).

What is recorded, who is on it, how long things are kept and what is never stored. The employer's view is in section 2.11.

What is recorded

  • Webinars: always, from start to end, unless the moderator stops it early.
  • Consultations: never. There is no recording control.
  • On a webinar recording: the presenters' video, sound and shared screen, and the voice of an attendee given the floor after they unmute. Attendees cannot send video, so no attendee is ever seen.
  • The notice is shown at registration, in the email, in the lobby and before an attendee unmutes.

What is never stored

  • Professional notes: the outcome form has no notes field.
  • The content of a consultation: no recording, no chat.
  • Join tokens: never written to the database or to logs.
  • Quick signals such as “I cannot hear you”: gone when the session ends.
  • Who voted for what in a poll.
  • Names or device names in technical reports.

How long data is kept

  • Appointments, sessions, outcomes and approval requests: 7 years.
  • Connection diagnostics and technical reports: 90 days.
  • Webinar messages and questions, polls: 12 months; then the link to the author is removed from messages.
  • Feedback and webinar registrations: 24 months.
  • Raw recording pieces: 30 days. The finished replay follows the content team's rules.
  • A shared location: deleted when the session ends.

These are defaults until the data protection assessment confirms them.

Where it lives, and help in a crisis

Calls run on Amazon Chime SDK in the Frankfurt region (eu-central-1); recordings are stored there, encrypted at rest. The video service receives random ids, never names or email addresses.

The crisis block lists Serbian numbers only, because NestSelf launches in Serbia: emergency 112, ambulance 194, Centar Srce 0800 300 303. Hungarian numbers come with a Hungarian launch.

The consultations and webinars described here are built and tested in the browser against the NestSelf API with a stand-in video service. These are the gaps that are known and accepted for now.

No measurement against AWS yet

The eight spikes have scripts and result templates but have not run in the dev account. Until they do, some behaviour rests on written assumptions: that the recording takes one meeting seat, that the recording shows the presenters only, and that a speaker in a large webinar keeps stable sound.

Certificates and the load test

Certificates of attendance and the load test before the first large webinar are not started. They belong to the first quarter after the pilot.

Phones and workshops

The mobile app does not open calls yet; on a phone, the employee uses the workspace app in the browser, without screen sharing. Small group workshops and replay captions come later. There are no live captions: the service does not support Serbian or Hungarian.

Service coordinators

Service coordinators cannot moderate webinars, book or cancel consultations until the founder decides how their access is scoped. Moderators today are client operations and the platform owner.

Replay hand-off

After a webinar, the hand-off to the content team is a pending note with the file's folder; the content system connects when its media interface exists. The email that tells presenters they were recorded is written but not sent. A presenter who joins after the recording started may be missing from it.

Expert photo and credentials

The waiting card shows the expert's first name and initial only; there is no source for the photo and credentials yet.

Webinar audience

There is no plan model yet, so “Every eligible organisation” reaches every active member of every active client organisation.

Hungarian hotlines

The crisis block lists Serbian numbers only.

Working days and holidays

Approval reminders and lapses count Monday to Friday in Belgrade time and do not know public holidays.

Support contact

The technical support address is a placeholder on a reserved domain until it is set for launch.
