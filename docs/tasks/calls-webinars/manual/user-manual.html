Consultations and webinars

Step-by-step guide. Each chapter follows one person through one task, screen by screen: joining a consultation, answering a follow-up, approving one, attending or running a webinar.

English edition. The product also ships in Serbian (Latin and Cyrillic) and Hungarian; every screen works the same in each language.

Who uses which app

  • Employees and HR use the workspace app.
  • Experts and NestSelf operations use the admin app.
  • Pick the tutorial for your task from the list below. Each one stands alone.

How to read a step

  • Buttons and fields are in bold green, like Join session. Messages the app shows are in quotes, like “Five minutes left.”
  • Each step shows the screen on a computer (left) and on a phone (right). Times are HH:MM and dates DATE where the app fills them in.
  • The people are fictional sample people: Jovan Petrović (employee), Milica Ristić (expert), Ivana Jovanović (HR), Nikola Đorđević (operations). Video is a drawn placeholder.

An employee meets a NestSelf expert in a private video session. Only the two of you are in the room, and nothing is recorded.

1

Open your appointments

In the workspace app, open Appointments. Upcoming lists the sessions to come; each row shows the date, the service, your expert's first name and a status badge.

2

Open the appointment

Until 15 minutes before the start the button reads Come back at HH:MM and cannot be selected. You can still Cancel appointment from here.

3

Join from 15 minutes before

When the window opens, Join session becomes active. Select it.

The join window

Joining opens 15 minutes before the start. A first join is possible until 10 minutes after the start; once you have joined, you can rejoin until the room closes, 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

4

Check your devices

“Check your camera and microphone” tests your camera, microphone, speaker and connection. Speak and watch the bar move, select Play test sound, change a device if needed, then Join now. The microphone must work; the camera is optional.

5

Optional: camera off or blurred background

Switch on Join with camera off to join without video, or Blur my background to hide the room behind you. Both are remembered on this device.

6

Wait for your expert

If you are first, the stage says “Your expert will join shortly” with the scheduled time and a few tips. If your expert is running late, the card tells you. The call starts when they join.

7

Talk

Your expert fills the screen; your own video sits in a corner. The bar has Mute, Turn camera off, Blur background, Devices, Full screen, the quick signals I cannot hear you and I cannot see you, and Leave. A consultation is never recorded.

8

On a phone

The main controls stay in the bar; More holds blur, full screen and the two quick signals.

9

Need help right now?

Crisis resources is always at the top. It opens the Serbian crisis numbers (112, 194, Centar Srce 0800 300 303) over the running call; on a phone, tap a number to call it. Close it with the cross.

10

Five minutes left

The timer shows the time elapsed and left. Five minutes before the end, “Five minutes left.” appears. The room closes 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

11

The session ends

When your expert ends the session, the stage says “The consultation has ended.” for five seconds, then the post-call screen opens. Leave at any time leaves only you.

12

Leave private feedback

Rate the session from 1 to 5 and add a comment if you like, then Send feedback, or Skip. “Your feedback is private and your employer will not see it.”

13

Report a technical problem

If something went wrong, Report a technical problem. Pick what failed; the dialog lists exactly what is sent (call length, reconnections, connection quality, browser, operating system) and nothing else. Send report.

What the app does on a bad connection, and what you see when the join window has closed.

1

A weak connection

The badge turns yellow and a banner says “Your connection is unstable. Video quality may drop for a moment.” Keep talking; the app lowers your video quality first.

2

Video paused, sound continues

After two poor measurements your outgoing video pauses: “Your video is paused due to connection quality. Audio continues.” It comes back by itself when the connection recovers.

3

Connection lost

The app shows “Connection lost. Reconnecting…” and tries for up to two minutes. If that fails, choose Rejoin or Contact support. Rejoining puts you back into the same session while the room is open.

4

You are too late

More than 10 minutes after the start, a first join is no longer possible: the page says “The join window for this session has closed.” and offers technical support. You also receive an email asking whether a technical problem kept you away.

5

The join is refused at the last moment

The server checks the window again when you select Join now. If it closed in the meantime, the device check shows “This session cannot be joined right now. Joining opens shortly before the start and closes 10 minutes after it; if you already joined, you can rejoin until shortly after the end.”

Your expert proposes a date and time; you confirm, suggest other times or decline. Some organisations ask an approver first.

1

A proposal arrives

After the call your expert can propose another session. It appears on the post-call screen, on the appointment page and in an email titled “A follow-up was proposed” Choose Confirm, Suggest other times or Decline.

2

Confirm

If your organisation books follow-ups without approval, the session is booked at once. If an approver decides first, the card says “Waiting for approval”

3

Or suggest other times

Give up to three windows in the appointment's time zone (Add another time adds one), then Send suggestions.

4

Your expert picks one

The card says “You suggested other times. The expert will choose one of them.” The status stays Follow-up proposed until the expert chooses; then it is booked, or waits for approval.

5

Or decline

Decline ends the proposal: “You declined this follow-up.” Your expert is told and can propose another time.

If your organisation approves follow-ups first

6

Waiting for the decision

The appointment shows Waiting for approval. The approver sees only your name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and the length. You and your expert are told when they decide.

7

Approved

The follow-up becomes an ordinary Confirmed appointment. Join it like the first one, from 15 minutes before the start.

8

Declined

The page shows the approver's short reason. Only you see it. Talk to your expert or HR if you still need the session.

If nothing happens
  • Nobody is assigned to approve for your team: confirming fails with “Your organisation has not assigned anyone to approve follow-ups yet. Contact your HR team.”
  • The approver does not decide in time: “The approval lapsed before the proposed time.”
  • The proposed time passes first: “The proposed time has passed. Your expert can propose a new one.”

An expert joins, shares a screen if needed, ends the session, records what happened and proposes a follow-up.

1

Open your consultations

In the admin app, open Consultations. You see only the consultations assigned to you: employee first name, service, time in the appointment's zone, status and outcome.

2

Open the consultation, tell the employee if you are late

Inside the join window the page offers Join session. If you are delayed, set the Estimated delay and Send running late; the employee sees it in the waiting card. Join runs the same device check as the employee's.

3

Your call screen

You have the employee's controls plus Share screen and End for everyone. If the employee shared a location, the card “Location the employee shared” shows it. Your crisis panel adds guidance for experts.

4

End for everyone

End for everyone asks “End the consultation for both of you?” Confirm with End consultation. The call closes for both of you and the outcome form opens.

5

Record the outcome

Choose one: Completed, The employee did not join, I could not join, Technical failure or Canceled, then Save outcome. There is no notes field: NestSelf stores no professional notes.

6

Propose a follow-up

After a completed outcome, pick the Date, Time and Duration and Send proposal. No free text. The card then says “Waiting for the employee to answer.”

If the employee suggests other times

Open the follow-up with Open the follow-up, choose one of their windows under Suggested times and Schedule. From there it is booked or goes to approval, as when the employee confirms.

An organisation can ask an approver to agree before a follow-up is booked. HR sets the policy; the approver for the employee's team decides each request from four facts.

1

Set the policy once

HR opens Follow-up approval in the employer space. Choose Book without approval (the default) or An approver decides first, then Save.

2

Decide a request

Approvers open Approvals. Each request shows the employee's name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and time, and the length. Nothing else. Select Approve or Decline.

3

Declining needs a reason

A short Reason is required. Only the employee sees it.

Deadlines
  • The request email goes to the approver and the alternate approver; both can decide. Nobody decides their own follow-up.
  • After 5 working days without a decision, both receive a reminder.
  • The request lapses at the earlier of the proposed start and 10 working days. It becomes Expired, and the employee is told.

A live talk by NestSelf experts. You watch and listen and are never on camera; every webinar is recorded, and only the presenters are on the recording unless you take the floor.

1

Find a webinar

Open Webinars. You see the webinars your organisation's audience includes, with the date, the presenters, the places left and the notice “This webinar is recorded; only the presenters are on the recording.”

2

Register

Open the webinar and select Register. A confirmation email follows with a private calendar entry; reminders come 24 hours and 1 hour before.

3

Come back when it starts

The page shows a countdown and Join webinar, which opens 15 minutes before the start. Cancel registration gives your place back until then.

4

The lobby

Join webinar first asks you to “Check your sound” (no camera is needed). Then you wait: “The hosts have not started yet. You join automatically when they start.” You join automatically when the hosts start.

5

Watch and listen

You see the presenters and any shared screen, with REC. Your controls: Mute the webinar sound, Raise hand, Full screen and Leave. There is no camera button and no list of attendees.

6

Write to the hosts

In Chat, choose Message or Question, write, and Send to the hosts. A moderator reviews it before anyone else sees it. Tick Show my first name with this message to sign it; otherwise it is published as Participant.

7

Vote in a poll

When the moderator opens a poll, pick an option and Vote. “Votes are counted without names.” Results appear as percentages when the poll closes.

Speaking

8

Raise your hand

Raise hand tells the hosts you would like to speak; Lower hand takes it back. The moderator sees your name; the presenters only see that a hand is up.

9

You have the floor

A dialog says “You can speak now, unmute to talk.” and reminds you that your voice is recorded. Your microphone stays off until you choose Unmute now; Not now keeps it off. Your camera never turns on.

10

Muted by a host, then asked to unmute

A host can mute you while you speak. If they ask you to speak again, you decide: Unmute or Stay muted. Nobody can unmute you but you.

11

In a large webinar

Above 250 attendees the audience is spread over linked rooms. When you are given the floor, your microphone is joined to the presenters' room for your turn, sound only. The dialog and controls are the same.

12

The end

When the hosts end the webinar you see “Thank you for joining” and “A replay will be available after review.” The recording goes to NestSelf's content team for review first.

13

If a webinar is canceled

You receive an email, and the page says “This webinar was canceled.” There is nothing to join.

Operations prepare the webinar: title, audience, capacity, presenters, moderator and schedule. Publishing opens registration.

1

Open the webinar list

In the admin app, open Webinars. Each webinar shows its date, status (Draft, Upcoming, Live now, Ended, Canceled) and capacity. Select New webinar.

2

Fill in the form

Title in at least one language, audience (Every eligible organisation, Selected organisations or Units of one organisation), capacity up to 1,000, the presenters, the moderator and the schedule. There is no recording switch: every webinar is recorded. Save draft.

3

Publish

The draft page lists the facts. Publish makes the webinar visible to the eligible employees, who can then register. Publishing needs: “Before publishing, add at least one presenter and a moderator, set a start in the future and choose an audience.”

4

Edit a draft or an upcoming webinar

Edit opens the same form. “The audience can be changed only while the webinar is a draft.” Save changes. A live, ended or canceled webinar cannot be changed.

5

Cancel

Cancel webinar asks “Cancel this webinar?” Registered employees are emailed, reminders stop, and it cannot be undone.

Capacity

Up to 250 attendees share one room. For a larger audience, up to 1,000, the platform adds linked rooms that receive only the presenters; attendees see no difference, and chat, polls and attendance work across them.

Presenters speak and share their screen. The moderator reviews messages, gives the floor, runs polls and can stop the recording.

1

Presenters open the live viewPresenter

From the webinar page, Open the live view up to 30 minutes before the start. After the device check (with an optional Test screen sharing), the lobby shows the registered count and Start webinar. Starting also starts the recording.

2

The moderator consoleModerator

The Moderation panel shows attendance, REC and Stop recording. Each raised hand has Give the floor. Each message waiting for review can be Published, edited, answered with Answer, or Dismissed.

3

While an attendee speaksModerator

The speaker appears under Speaking now with Mute, Ask to unmute and Take the floor back. Only the attendee can unmute themselves.

Polls and ending

Under Prepare a poll, write a question and two to six options, Add poll, then Open poll; Close poll publishes the results without names. End webinar for everyone ends the webinar and the recording; otherwise the room closes 15 minutes after the scheduled end.

4

Stop the recording earlyModerator

Only the moderator can. Stop recording asks “Stop the recording now?” The rest is not recorded and recording cannot be restarted.

5

Afterwards: attendanceOperations

The webinar page shows Attendance: registered, joined and peak counts, then one row per registration without names, and the recording state (Being processed, Ready, Handed to the content team or Failed).

NestSelf operations book and cancel consultations for the client companies they coordinate.

1

The operations list

Client operations open Consultations: every consultation of the organisations you coordinate, with Expert and Organisation columns and Filters by organisation, status and dates.

2

Book a consultation

Select New consultation. Choose the Organisation, the Employee, the Expert and the Service, then the Date, Start time and End time in Europe/Belgrade. Book consultation. Reminders are scheduled at once.

3

Cancel a consultation

On the consultation page, Cancel appointment asks “Cancel this appointment?” Both participants receive an email, and a call that is already running closes.

Recording

  • Webinars: always, from start to end, unless the moderator stops it early. Attendees are told at registration, in the email, in the lobby and before they unmute.
  • On the recording: the presenters' video, sound and shared screen, and the voice of an attendee who took the floor. No attendee is ever seen.
  • Consultations: never. There is no recording control.

Never stored

  • Professional notes: the outcome form has no notes field.
  • What was said in a consultation: no recording, no chat.
  • Who voted for what in a poll; names or device names in technical reports.
  • Quick signals such as “I cannot hear you”: gone when the session ends.

What your employer sees

  • Nothing about your consultations: not that you booked, joined or missed one, not its topic, outcome, feedback or length.
  • Nothing about which webinars you attended or what you wrote in them.
  • If your organisation approves follow-ups: your name, “Follow-up session with a NestSelf expert”, the date and the length. That is all.

How long data is kept

  • Appointments, sessions, outcomes and approval requests: 7 years.
  • Connection diagnostics and technical reports: 90 days.
  • Webinar messages and polls: 12 months. Feedback and registrations: 24 months.
  • Raw recording pieces: 30 days. A shared location: deleted when the session ends.

Defaults until the data protection assessment confirms them. Calls run in the Frankfurt region; the video service receives random ids, never names.
