# User-story catalog: consultations and webinars on Amazon Chime SDK

Epic `NS-CALLS-01`. User-story catalog v1.1, 24 October 2026. Revised the same day after the founder's answers (F-CW-01 to F-CW-10) and decisions D-CW-34 to D-CW-38. 102 stories (42 CALL, 37 WBN, 5 WKS, 18 PLT). Grounded in the decision record (`02-decisions.md` v1.1), the UX research (`research/04-ux-practices.md`), the compliance and consent research (`research/03-compliance-consent-cost.md`), the webinar recording research (`research/02-webinars-recording-captions.md`), the codebase survey (`research/06-codebase-survey.md`) and the product flows specification (`sources/product-flows-and-user-stories-v0.1.md`).

## How to read this catalog

Each story has a unique id with a prefix that names its domain: `CALL-nn` for consultations (employee, expert, appointment spine and mobile), `WBN-nn` for webinars (attendee, presenter, moderator, operations), `WKS-nn` for workshops and `PLT-nn` for platform, support, operations and non-functional concerns.

**Priority.** P0: required for the pilot. P1: first quarter after the pilot. P2: later.

**Milestone.** M0: foundation and spikes. M1: consultations with the end-of-call follow-up (D-CW-37). M2: webinars live up to 250 attendees, always recorded, with the replay handoff. M3: webinar scale beyond 250 (replication), certificates and the load test. M4: mobile join, workshops and replay captions.

**Testability rule.** Every acceptance criterion is written so that a reviewer can write a test from it. Observable outcomes name the HTTP status code, UI state, error code, audit event, Socket.IO event or data boundary that a test asserts. If a criterion cannot be tested, it does not belong here.

**Grounding column.** Lists the decision ids (`D-CW-nn`), UX research ids (`UX-nn`), compliance fact ids (`LAW-nn`), webinar research ids (`WEB-nn`) and the product-flows story id it traces to, where applicable.

## Persona summary

| Persona | Permission template | Gains in this epic | Scope |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Employee | `employee` | Nothing new (already holds `consultation.join`, `event.register`, `appointment.cancel`) | Own appointments, own webinar registrations; never on camera in webinars |
| Expert | `expert` | Gains `event.present` (already holds `consultation.join`, `delivery.record`, `appointment.propose`; `event.present` exists in the action catalogue but is not yet in the template) | Own assigned appointments, own webinar presenting assignments |
| Service coordinator | `service_coordinator` | `event.moderate` | Assigned queues, moderation of assigned webinars |
| Webinar presenter | `expert` (GAP-H02 default) | Gains `event.present` through the expert template update | Assigned webinar occurrence |
| Moderator | `service_coordinator`, `client_operations` or `platform_owner` | `event.moderate` | Assigned or all webinar occurrences depending on template |
| NestSelf operations | `platform_owner`, `client_operations` | `event.manage`, `event.moderate` | All organisations (platform_owner) or assigned organisations (client_operations) |
| Content operator | `content_publisher` | `event.manage` | Content collections and webinar occurrences |
| Support operator | `support_operator` | Nothing new (`support.troubleshoot`) | Assigned support cases, redacted diagnostics |
| Approver | Organisation-scoped template with `approval.decide` | `approval.decide` (follow-up approval items, with the self-approval rule and the alternate approver) | Follow-up approval items for employees whose organisational unit is covered by the approver's authority; sees employee name, date and duration, never the category, specialty or content |
| HR administrator | Organisation-scoped template with `program.overview` | `program.overview` (sets the follow-up approval policy on the organisation) | Organisation settings including the follow-up approval policy (`automatic` or `manager_approval`) |
| Organisation-scoped roles (employer owner, team manager, employer finance, reporting user) | Organisation-scoped templates (template keys are those in `packages/contracts/src/templates.ts`) | Nothing. These roles cannot join calls, view participant lists, read messages or access individual attendance. | Organisation-scoped aggregate data only, subject to k-anonymity threshold of 5 |
