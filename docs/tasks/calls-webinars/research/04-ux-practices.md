## Recommended flows for NestSelf

### 1:1 Consultation

#### Pre-join (employee side)
1. **Appointment reminder** (SMS + push, 24h and 1h before): neutral wording ("Your NestSelf session starts in 1 hour"). ICS file with `CLASS:PRIVATE` and SUMMARY "NestSelf session". Push notification on lock screen shows only "Upcoming appointment" until unlocked.
2. **Join link active** 15 minutes before the scheduled time.
3. **Device check screen**: camera preview (self-view), microphone level indicator, speaker test button, network quality indicator (green/yellow/red). Options: "Join with camera off", "Join audio only". Device selection dropdowns (camera, mic, speaker).
4. **Browser permission handling**: if denied, show browser-specific inline instructions with illustrations. Do not proceed until at least microphone access is granted.
5. **Consent step** (if recording is enabled for the session): "This session may be recorded for [purpose]. You can decline recording without affecting your session." Checkbox or explicit button. Skip if recording is not configured for this session type.
6. **Location prompt** (first session or if address changed): "Please confirm your current location in case of emergency." Text field, pre-filled from profile.
7. **Waiting state**: "Your expert will join shortly." Show expert's first name, photo, and credentials. Display provider status (available / on another call / running late + ETA). Show a "Good to know" section with session tips. If the expert sends a "running late" message, display it inline.
8. **Late-join messaging**: if the employee joins after the scheduled time, show "Your session started at HH:MM. You can still join for the remaining time." If the window has closed (>10 min after), show "The join window for this session has closed. [Rebook] [Contact support]".
9. **No-show (employee side)**: if the employee has not joined by T+10 min, the expert marks no-show. Within 48 hours, the system sends a follow-up: "We noticed you missed your session. Was this due to a technical issue? [Report technical problem] [Rebook]".

**Decision: NestSelf should set the window to 15 min before / 10 min after, matching the product spec.**

#### In-call (1:1)
1. **Layout**: speaker view -- expert's video fills the screen, employee's self-view is a small movable PIP in the corner. Employee can hide self-view. Expert sees the same layout (employee large, self PIP).
2. **Controls bar** (bottom): Mute/Unmute | Camera on/off | Background blur | Device switch | Fullscreen | Chat (limited) | Leave.
   - **Expert-only controls**: "End for everyone", "Extend session" (adds 15 or 30 min), crisis resource shortcut.
   - **Chat**: available for URL sharing and "I can't hear you" messages only. Chat is not stored after the session ends. Display a notice: "Chat messages are not saved."
3. **Connection quality indicator**: persistent icon in the corner (strong/unstable/poor). If quality degrades: first reduce video resolution, then pause video with a banner "Video paused -- audio only due to network quality". Resume video automatically when quality recovers.
4. **Reconnection**: on disconnect, show "Connection lost -- reconnecting..." with a spinner. If reconnection fails after 15 seconds, show "Unable to reconnect. [Rejoin] [Switch to phone call]". Keep the session alive for 2 minutes to allow rejoin.
5. **Timer**: show elapsed time and remaining time. At 5 minutes remaining, show a dismissable alert to both parties: "5 minutes remaining in your session." At 0 minutes, show "Session time has ended" but do not auto-disconnect; the expert decides.
6. **Crisis access**: a small "Crisis resources" button visible at all times (unobtrusive, e.g. a shield icon). Clicking it shows the crisis hotline number and a "Contact emergency services" option for the expert. The expert can use masked calling (outbound call from the platform, not their personal number) without ending the video session.
7. **Recording indicator**: if recording is active, show a persistent red "REC" badge. If recording starts mid-session, re-prompt consent.
8. **Accessibility**: all controls keyboard-operable with visible focus; mute/camera state announced via `aria-live`; live captions available (toggle in controls); reduced motion: opacity-only transitions <=50 ms.
9. **Mobile (390px)**: full-screen remote video, floating PIP, bottom control bar with 44x44px tap targets, portrait default. Controls collapse into a "More" menu if space is tight (blur, device switch, fullscreen behind the overflow).

**Decision: chat is minimal (not a messaging channel). NestSelf should NOT enable full messaging during counselling sessions -- it creates clinical documentation risk and distraction. A URL-sharing and "can't hear you" fallback is sufficient.**

**Decision: background blur should be offered but NOT enforced by default. The employee can choose in the pre-join screen and the choice is remembered.**

#### Post-call
1. **Expert outcome capture** (required before next session): Completed / No-show / Technical failure / Employee cancelled. Free-text notes field (private to expert, not visible to employer). Connection metadata (duration, reconnects, quality scores) auto-attached.
2. **Employee feedback** (optional, appears 5 seconds after call ends): "How was your session?" 1-5 stars + optional text. Explicit note: "Your feedback is private and your employer will not see it." Skip button prominent.
3. **Rejoin**: if the call dropped within the join window, show "Rejoin your session" button for 5 minutes after the drop. After the window closes, show "This session has ended. [Report a technical issue] [Book a new session]".
4. **Technical failure path**: employee can report a technical issue; support sees connection metadata (join time, duration, packet loss, browser, OS, reconnect count) but never session content. The expert also flags technical failure in outcome capture.

### Webinar (attendee)
1. **Registration**: attendee registers with name and email. Receives a confirmation email with an ICS file (neutral title: "NestSelf Webinar", no mention of wellbeing category). Reminder emails at 24h and 1h before.
2. **Join page** (active 10 min before start): branded holding page with event title, presenter name and bio, start time, countdown. "Your camera and microphone will be off during this session."
3. **Lobby**: attendee waits; sees the countdown and event details. No attendee list visible. No attendee names shown anywhere.
4. **During webinar**: attendee sees the presenter's video/screen share. No attendee tiles, no attendee names, no attendee count visible to other attendees. Controls: volume, captions toggle, fullscreen, Q&A, leave.
5. **Q&A**: attendees submit written questions. Option to submit anonymously (default: anonymous). Moderator reviews and approves before questions appear to all. Approved questions show without the author's name unless the author opted in.
6. **Hand-raise / ask to speak**: attendee can raise hand; moderator promotes them to speaker (mic + camera enabled temporarily); after their question, moderator demotes them back to attendee.
7. **Polls**: embedded polls appear during the presentation; results shown live (aggregated).
8. **Captions**: auto-generated, matching the session locale.
9. **Leaving**: attendee can leave at any time; no exit message shown to others.
10. **Replay**: available after the presenter/admin reviews and approves the recording. Attendee receives an email with the replay link. Viewing the replay also counts toward the attendance certificate if applicable.
11. **Certificate of attendance**: generated automatically if the attendee attended >= 60% of the live session OR completed the replay. Emailed as a PDF with a QR verification code. Available for re-download from the employee's session history.
12. **Mobile (390px)**: portrait-oriented video stream, Q&A and polls in a bottom sheet or tab, 44px tap targets.

**Decision: attendee anonymity is absolute in webinars. No attendee list, no names in Q&A unless opted in, no mutual video/audio. This directly satisfies the NestSelf requirement that employees of client companies must not see each other.**

**Potential conflict: the certificate of attendance reveals that an employee attended a webinar. If the webinar topic is sensitive (e.g. "Managing anxiety"), the certificate title should be generic or the employee should choose whether to request one.**

### Webinar (presenter / moderator)
1. **Presenter preview**: device check (camera, mic, screen share test), session details, attendee count (number only, no names visible to presenter). "Start webinar" button.
2. **During webinar**: presenter sees their own video, screen share controls, Q&A panel (approved questions only, unless also acting as moderator), polls panel, timer, attendance count.
3. **Moderator queue**: moderator sees all submitted questions with author name (or "Anonymous"). Can approve, edit, dismiss, or answer privately. Can promote an attendee to speaker (mic + camera) and demote them after.
4. **Muting**: moderator can mute any promoted speaker. Cannot unmute without the speaker's consent (they receive an "unmute request").
5. **Screen share**: presenter can share screen or specific window. A "you are sharing your screen" indicator is always visible.
6. **Recording**: a red "REC" indicator is visible to all when recording is active. The recording is NOT auto-published.
7. **Ending**: presenter clicks "End webinar for all." A confirmation dialog prevents accidental ending. All attendees see "This webinar has ended. A replay will be available soon."
8. **Post-webinar**: presenter/admin reviews the recording, trims start/end, and publishes. Publication triggers the replay email to attendees.
9. **Analytics**: post-event, the presenter sees: peak attendance, average attendance, questions submitted, polls completed, engagement score. Individual attendee names are visible only to the moderator/admin (for follow-up), not to the presenter.

**Decision: presenter sees count only, not names. Moderator sees names for operational reasons (promoting speakers, following up). This separation protects attendee privacy while enabling moderation.**

### Group workshop (<=25 participants)
1. **Pre-join**: same device check as 1:1 consultation. Camera ON by default (workshop is participatory). Background blur offered.
2. **Layout**: gallery view (all participants visible in a grid, up to 25 tiles). Active speaker highlighted. Facilitator can spotlight or pin participants.
3. **Controls**: same as 1:1 plus: hand-raise, reactions. Facilitator has: mute-all, unmute request, spotlight, remove participant.
4. **Screen share**: facilitator and promoted participants can share. Stage view activates automatically when sharing.
5. **Chat**: enabled for group discussion (unlike 1:1 counselling, workshops benefit from written interaction). Chat is logged for the facilitator's notes but not shared with the employer.
6. **Timer**: session timer visible. 5-minute warning.
7. **Post-session**: facilitator marks completion. Optional group feedback prompt (anonymous, aggregated).
8. **Privacy**: participants see each other's first names and video (this is expected in a workshop setting). The employer does NOT see who attended -- only that a workshop occurred.
9. **Mobile**: gallery view adapts to show 4-6 tiles on a 390px screen with scroll for additional participants.

**Decision: workshops are the only format where participants see each other. This must be clearly communicated at registration: "This is a group session. Other participants will see your name and video."**

## Could not verify / open questions

1. **Chime SDK pre-join React component**: no evidence of a ready-made React component for the meeting readiness checker. NestSelf will likely need to wrap the JavaScript readiness-check APIs in a custom React component.
2. **Doxy.me audio-only join**: could not confirm whether Doxy.me offers a "join without camera" option at the pre-join step; the audio-only fallback was found in an October 2024 release note but details are thin.
3. **Zoom for Healthcare degraded-mode audio fallback**: no official Zoom documentation was found confirming an automatic audio-only fallback when video quality degrades. Doxy.me and Twilio describe this pattern, but Zoom's implementation is unverified.
4. **GDPR-specific recording consent for health data**: all recording consent guidance found is HIPAA-focused. GDPR Article 9 (special category data) and Article 6 lawful basis for recording telehealth sessions in the EU/Serbia need separate legal research. See 03-compliance-consent-cost.md.
5. **ICS CLASS:PRIVATE behavior across calendar clients**: the `CLASS:PRIVATE` property in ICS files is documented in RFC 5545, but whether all major calendar apps (Google Calendar, Apple Calendar, Outlook) actually hide the event title from colleagues on the lock screen is unverified. Google Calendar in particular may ignore `CLASS`.
6. **Oliva platform**: no information found on Oliva's video consultation UX, privacy practices, or employer data sharing. The platform may have rebranded or been acquired.
7. **Nielsen Norman Group video call UX research**: no NNG research was found on video call or telehealth UX design patterns. The academic sources from Leporini et al. (2022) are the closest available.
8. **Screenshot prevention effectiveness**: `FLAG_SECURE` on Android blocks standard screenshots but not all screen recording methods. iOS has no equivalent API for WebRTC views. The practical effectiveness in a browser-based WebRTC app is low -- a notice/warning is more realistic than prevention.
9. **Certificate of attendance and sensitive topics**: if a webinar topic reveals a wellbeing category, the certificate itself becomes a privacy leak. No platform was found to address this; NestSelf should consider letting employees opt in to certificates rather than auto-generating them.
10. **Minimum group size for aggregate reporting**: Unmind withholds data for "a few" people but does not publish the exact threshold. TechRadar recommends 5. The k-anonymity threshold NestSelf should use needs a formal decision (5, 10, or higher depending on org size).
